## What turns an observation into a decision

Four retinotopic motor outputs receive signals from the stimulus onset, prospective outlet activity, context and recent somatic firing. Two inherited 32-bit programs choose three inputs per cell, their local Boolean response, recurrent routing and the quorum for opening the eye. Different genotypes can ignore evidence, collect everything or act selectively. No error label, physical destination or prescribed uncertainty threshold enters that decision. Actual pigmentation, body heading and learned synaptic contacts affect the input. Full observation costs one unit of energy. Both genes use ordinary mutable pigment loci and reuse the animal's existing neural state. [Gaze and reach circuit](../include/axion/fovea.hpp)

Two wet, stocked inlets can also be probed with a limb. The same circuit receives the two **onsets**, and the stronger discharge recruits a hand. In the field an initiated movement dislodges one unit of existing litter. It can remain at the inlet, travel or meet a full destination. Neighbors can pay to observe it and modify their own synapses. In a spoken inquiry, activity evoked by the heard route returns to the gaze circuit. A new discharge can initiate further tests. The animal can stop before exhausting either the rack or its energy. An independent CUDA release subsequently delivers the held load or diverts it. The ordinary question and reply use the resident's learned acoustic contacts. ## Execution and reproduction

Eight four-cell input populations fit in one 32-bit register. Gene-controlled shifts select synaptic inputs. Blackwell's `PRMT` evaluates four local truth tables together. Passive sampling has a separate CUDA entry point. The optional gaze ledger costs 5,120 bytes per habitat. The field adds **zero persistent bytes per resident** for either gaze or tracer probes. Full raw sampling allocates a bounded diagnostic buffer only when requested.

Five alternating measurements used 7,705 actual resident circuits and 256 thirteen-stage forecasts per resident on the RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell Laptop. Median confirmation times were **17.464 ms** for passive viewing, **21.255 ms** for voluntary gaze and **29.064 ms** for two-inlet investigations. The latter two fell from **23.461** and **33.578 ms** after specializing the CUDA kernels and packing the retinal circuit. Every common per-resident result column matched its reference. These rates are **112.9**, **92.8** and **67.9 million** held-out forecasts/s. Habitat metrics measure complete simulation steps separately. ## Biological motivation and remaining limits

Recent mouse experiments measured shifts in visual sampling when occlusion made a judgment harder. A separate orbitofrontal study measured how much water mice would sacrifice to acquire information. We used these findings to motivate charging for observation and testing against equal-cost controls. Human experiments on sampling and commitment motivated giving each animal the option to stop with resources left. A November 2026 reversal-learning study examined how goal specificity and uncertainty affect decisions. Here we can physically exchange the working goal and test whether the evidence gathered is useful for the requested outcome. These are design inferences. [Visual uncertainty and active sensing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13388018/) · [Value of information in mice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02377-y) · [Sampling and commitment in humans](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42492665/) · [Goal specificity and uncertainty](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301051126002188)

The environment provides the paired source affordance, timing of available opportunities, pigment channels and material law. The speech interface supplies a bounded learned grammar and a register for the heard itinerary. Unrestricted language, invention of the experimental apparatus and human-level intelligence remain unproven.
