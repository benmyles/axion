# Tools whose value changes after they have been learned

A pressure tool can leave its vibration and relief on a different grain. A pupil can see a sister impression used dry, taste a newly adjusted reservoir with unrelated bare probes, and choose the working copy on its first attempt. The hardware carries two independently writable sensory projections in spare bits of the existing spiking tissue. In the free watershed, access to information and the cost of obtaining it limit how much of the nursery result survives.

## Scientific basis

A 2026 mouse study followed hippocampal reward representations over weeks. Some activity moved from the reward toward events that preceded it. A human contingent-learning experiment found evidence for the identity of earlier causal choices in hippocampal and lateral orbitofrontal signals when intervening choices separated action and outcome. Our engineering inference was to encode a sensory prediction separately from a currently valuable consequence. Revaluing a consequence can then change a tool choice without trying that tool at the new reservoir. The four-element codes, binary contact laws and indices are design choices. [Mouse reward representations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41535460/) · [Human credit assignment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40231604/)

A 2026 chimpanzee study found that demonstrator rank and payoff could interact. One group showed no significant preference between the demonstrated behaviors. A child experiment manipulated whether a learner could see a tool error and its consequence. These studies motivated different channels for a bystander, an active hand and a tasted result. Human experiments on paid information search also motivated varying the physical price of a probe. Each Axion intervention records what evidence reached the learner and how much movement it cost. [Chimpanzee choices and demonstrations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13582275/) · [Children observing a tool error](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jecp.2025.106338) · [Information search costs](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/judgment-and-decision-making/article/adapting-to-information-search-costs-in-samplebased-decisions/954EF745FC16B0139B12063BE4F78A5E)

A 2026 macro-level model found open-ended growth under a combination of cultural retention, an expanding search space and resource production. Axion tests one part of that hypothesis. An impression can physically alter another carrier and gain access to a finite resource in an unfamiliar location. The reported runs establish copying and learning in a bounded environment. Open-ended growth in technological complexity remains unverified. [Model of cultural evolution and expanding search spaces](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41987729/)

## What can happen in the world

A limb can press a held grain, a nearby mineral or a willing neighbor’s tool. The visible cue depends on its optical band and the exposed face. Pressure on a bare face produces a vibration determined by its material. A marked face produces its retained vibration. Pressing two distinct surfaces together in water can emboss a mirrored face on the receiver. The receiver records the current mechanical contact and whether it produced food. A fresh copy can act as a die for another copy. The organism never receives the identity of the maker.

Groundwater loads a slit with existing carbon. An aligned pressure stroke releases a limited volume into fruit. Feeding pores determine whether it enters the animal. The field accounts for remaining sediment, released carbon, swallowed food and movement heat. In laminated beds, regional geology slowly anneals exposed wet crystals. Hardening a bent face preserves the directions of its ports, its charge and its mass. The local bank height and the orientation of a worked grain change the slit. In tidal strata the phase also turns every 128 ticks, with a different offset in each vein.

An animal can spend additional energy to strike the inlet rim or either candidate. The rim’s pitch depends on the physical slit and the inertia of its fibre lining. The pupil has to learn how this relates to food. A tool’s diagnostic tap reveals its vibration before the final load-bearing stroke. Inherited truth tables gate each tap using current ambiguity and cost. Some genotypes spend their reserves indiscriminately. Pressure trials compare the same acquisition movements with their returns displaced. The receipt distinguishes paid taps, a change of motor choice, the actual carbon and the heat.

## The learning circuit

The writable state adds **32 binary contacts in zero additional per-resident bytes**. Sixteen link visible tool receptors to vibration cells. Sixteen link water or rim receptors to vibration cells modulated by the arrival of food. The physical storage uses the upper halves of two words already present in each clay cortex. Acquiring, swapping or reinstating the older acoustic assemblies preserves these contacts. A newly born animal starts with its own empty contact matrices. There are additional counters per CUDA work block.

Coincidence, the previous synaptic state, and either the source of a movement or a nutritional event address inherited transition tables. The genome also controls the sign and strength of competing inputs. Evolution can retain, weaken, invert or ignore an association. The actuating circuit combines the predicted vibration set with its current value set. Real physical evidence can replace the prediction for a finger that made a diagnostic tap. Earlier clay readers can also learn from a still-audible mark they encounter after the maker has left.

A detached two-tool chooser compiles the rank of four visible cues in each of four bath contexts into **four bytes** per resident. It preserves ties. This kernel measures prospective choices from fixed synapses. A living watershed uses the writable tissue and also pays for materials, physics, growth, sensory acquisition and reproduction. ## Speech from a resident

Resident 30 from the first independently tested nursery was selected among 306 living animals with eyes, limbs, feeding pores and working reserves. Copies of its genotype earned all 128 points on a separate physical screen for tool choice and sensory discrimination. Its human word-learning rule was bred separately. It received 384 visible contact captions and 180 witnessed questions whose single answer was a grounded word. The combined word bank has 32 slots and retains 48 sentence examples. Several noun positions and all equal-numbered bath–tool pairs were withheld from the question curriculum.

In 32 fresh held-out wheels the pupil correctly named the new tool **128/128**, the connecting vibration **128/128** and the vibration referred to by the follow-up **128/128**. It withheld all 128 conflicting claims. After seeing the tools without tasting the revised water it could still name their vibrations. After tasting with the tools unseen it could still name the bath’s useful vibration. Both experiences were required to choose and name the new working tool. Leaving the earlier water valuation in place elicited the old tool on all 128 trials. Clearing the preceding spoken reference caused 128 silences on the follow-up.

A second vocabulary assigns different names and word orders and replicates the result. A pupil without word tuition made no spoken tool identification. These are two vocabularies taught to the same selected physical phenotype. The test process supplies questions and transcribes the emitted words. Its grading keys remain outside the inference kernel. In the live session the first instrument is **flint**, then **ivory** after new blind samples. Work with ivory returns 18 carbon units. A press, a dry observation or a change of inlet resets the current conversational referent. The English demonstration supports bounded relational predictions and a grounded follow-up. General language and human-child-level intelligence remain open. ## Reproduce the experiments

The native build on this laptop uses the NVIDIA driver and Clang’s CUDA device compiler. Python runs the independent studies and grades their output. Kernel inference, physical interaction and developmental selection run on the GPU.

```sh
make -j4 CXX=/usr/bin/g++ CUDA_CLANG=/usr/bin/clang++ NATIVE=1 CUDA_ARCH=sm_120
make test-press CXX=/usr/bin/g++ CUDA_CLANG=/usr/bin/clang++ NATIVE=1 CUDA_ARCH=sm_120
mkdir -p pressure-study
python3 tools/press-study.py nursery --practice observed --output pressure-study/observed
python3 tools/press-study.py nursery --practice mixed --output pressure-study/mixed
python3 tools/press-study.py nursery --practice mixed --broods finite --output pressure-study/finite
python3 tools/press-study.py watershed --practice mixed --trials 1024 --output pressure-study/beds --keep pressure-study/kilnbank.flood
cmp examples/kilnbank.flood pressure-study/kilnbank.flood
python3 tools/press-feel.py --output pressure-study/feel
python3 tools/press-feel.py --source examples/kilnbank.flood --seeds 78307 --extend 512 --horizons 256 2048 8192 --arms live quiet displaced --output pressure-study/wide --keep pressure-study/first-wide.flood
./axion floodplain --load pressure-study/first-wide.flood --steps 8192 --save pressure-study/upwelling.flood
cmp examples/upwelling.flood pressure-study/upwelling.flood
```

The individual used in the two speech registers comes from the same recorded physical screen in each reproduction. The first command breeds a population. The second observes newborn copies on eight independent tables and exports the original resident with its own body and acquisitions. The language study holds its physical synapses fixed while it learns the public words. Blind sampling and watching regain their ordinary plasticity in the questions.

```sh
./axion floodplain --load examples/reedbed.flood --press live --press-seasons 192 --press-population 1024 --press-founders varied --press-seed 36871 --steps 0 --save pressure-study/selected.flood
./axion floodplain --load pressure-study/selected.flood --press-scholar pressure-study/pupil.mind --press-seed 804151 --steps 0
./axion ground --load pressure-study/pupil.mind --origin random --generations 48 --population 64 --worlds 8 --episodes 1024 --crowd 4 --dialect english --probes 128 --save pressure-study/word-pupil.mind
python3 tools/press-language.py --speaker pressure-study/word-pupil.mind --save pressure-study/reader.mind --output pressure-study/river.csv
cmp examples/wheel-reader.mind pressure-study/reader.mind
python3 tools/press-language.py --speaker pressure-study/word-pupil.mind --register fen --save pressure-study/fen.mind --output pressure-study/fen.csv
python3 tools/press-language.py --speaker pressure-study/word-pupil.mind --stations 8 --output pressure-study/untaught.csv
```

The optional field controls are `--press live|still|no-casting|alone|no-taste|unguided`, `--press-geology mixed|veins|tidal` and `--press-feel quiet|live|displaced|stiff`. A retired or introduced learning law is visible in the saved genotype. `--press-census` supplies a detached comparison on the current bodies and tools. On a mouthful, a blocked pore can release carbon into the local tray. The batch and the conversational inlet use the same physical operations and can be replayed without the scoring words.
