# Two marks and a destination neither animal had seen

Two animals born in the slow-pulse watershed learned to distinguish four food locations after their environment showed them only three. In a saved lifetime with the southeast cue withheld throughout rearing, the pair found the southeast feeder on **920 of 1,024** fresh frozen tests. The two other arms of the experiment matter. The result occurred in 33 of 256 independent lifetimes of the selected pair, and it occurred in zero of 256 when both sensory inputs were routed to both motor branches. The inherited animals projected different input axes to the two branches. ## The physical and neural channel

The sender sees the location of a stocked feeder and can press either, both or neither of two independent closed junctions. It leaves. A receiver presses both junctions and sees their two actual pressure readings. It chooses among four feeders. The original assay pays one portion to each animal only on reaching the stocked feeder. It gives no separate reward for getting a coordinate right. The receiver has no view of the writer or the food cue.

Two motor branches can read separate, overlapping, both or neither of two optical inputs. Two bits in each innervated neural program specify which physically expressed inputs enter its branch. A branch learns from its own movement, sensory input, bodily spike response and the shared feeding outcome. Sixteen local sensory–motor addresses need two signed bits each, a total of **four learned bytes per animal**. The same inherited local truth table and somatic release pathway act at both branches. Anatomy, sensory projection, response thresholds and exploration depend on the two individual genomes. The two spatial axes, feeder geometry, available effectors and learning substrate are engineered.

A reliable history meets two requirements at each of the four food locations. At least 75% of the choices must reach the stocked feeder. At least 75% of the sender’s gestures must agree on one mark pattern, with four different patterns across the four locations. For a separable mapping, changing either coordinate alone changes only one of the two marks. A joint mapping can change both marks. These criteria are external measurements and never reach either animal during learning. Work on combinations of bonobo calls motivated measuring separately which parts of a signal carry meaning. This artificial task tests systematic recombination in a much smaller domain. [Berthet and colleagues, Science 2025](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adv1170)

## Two acquired codes in the same bodies

After 4,096 feeding opportunities in four independent histories, 130 of 2,048 sampled brook pairs produced at least one reliable history. Their 309 reliable histories included 127 separable and 182 joint codes. With silenced updates, unrelated metabolic feedback or an inert medium the same brook population produced zero reliable histories. In the changing coastal population the two-port task also yielded zero reliable histories under the full-food protocol. [Population and intervention counts](compound-signs/populations.csv) · [Every brook pair](compound-signs/four-brook-4096-4-native-food-active.tsv.gz)

Eighty-three brook pairs produced both code types across the four histories. We took the first such pair in census order, slots 7218 and 1723, and the first qualifying history of each type. Both animals started each history with the same inherited body and the same initial state. History 0 produced a joint code. History 1 produced a separable code. Each of 1,024 fresh test fixtures then received four matched alternative food cues with identical receiver state and motor noise.

The joint writer’s most frequent marks for northwest, northeast, southwest and southeast were wait, both, second and first. The separable writer’s were second, wait, both and first. A changed learned history therefore changed which physical gesture carried a particular location. Erasing just one mark limited the reader to at most one half of the balanced choices. ## A completely omitted visual cue

The initial omitted-southeast screen found one qualifying pair among 2,048 later slow-pulse pairs. Slots 11672 and 9414 were born at ticks 153,584 and 153,355. Both inherited the same crossed projection, encoded as 6. Their first branch reads the second input and their second branch reads the first. The four sign meanings remained free to develop. Only that pair was selected for the replication. An aptitude-selected specimen establishes what the population contains and cannot establish how common the result is. ## Resources and social partners

The nutrient variant sends two independently coloured resources down intersecting rows and columns. Four feeders hold a finite stock. A receiver choosing an adjacent feeder may obtain one of the two nutrients. In the separated-taste treatment, existing stomach genes determine which nutrient can activate each branch’s local metabolic signal. The pooled treatment delivers the same physical supplies but collapses the experienced flavours into one metabolic signal. A weaning option makes the resource distribution concentrate after a specified number of feeding opportunities.

The result depended on ecology and lineage. With the same separable adult sender and the southeast cue withheld, the older nursery produced 127 of 2,048 juvenile pairs with a reliable life under separated tastes, 17 under pooled tastes and 70 when food existed only at the confluence. The starting brook produced 100, 3 and 174. In the coastal population, separated tastes produced three qualifying juvenile pairs, compared with zero in the other two treatments. A trained adult had previously encountered the fourth cue in these tutor studies. Only the juvenile was unexposed. ## Frozen GPU inference

Four adjacent CUDA lanes evaluate four possible worlds sharing a physical fixture and a receiver’s motor noise. They exchange readouts in registers with a warp shuffle. Each complete choice, two single-port erasures, both-port erasure, sensory observation, feeding result and energy cost fit in a two-byte receipt. A separate scalar CUDA path recomputes the same receipt for comparison. On the laptop’s RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell, all **4,194,304** receipts matched. Across seven alternating measurements the scalar median was 1.24525 ms and the cooperative median was 0.515936 ms, a **2.41×** speedup. The device reported 33 registers and zero thread-local bytes per lane. Timing covers this frozen readout with 1,024 fixture records reused across the batch. Habitat stepping, online plasticity, I/O and spoken encounters have separate timings.
