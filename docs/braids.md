# Braids

A speaker starts with four distinguishable floats. It hears a multiword request, predicts how its arrangement should change, and pays for a sequence of physical pulls. Another animal can witness the work. They can disagree about the result when a linkage jams or a view is obscured. In the released interview, both the words for the pigments and the ways to ask about an event were acquired during the creatures’ lifetimes.

The free-living habitat has reached tick 351,616. It contains 72,365 residents, 4,642,880 expressed somatic spiking cells and 262,144 independently movable carriers of food. The wild watershed, selected workshop populations and taught individuals have distinct experimental histories. [Watershed measurements](braids/watershed.csv)

## Speak to a saved animal

Run the first command to type a conversation. The second plays a recorded sequence using the same interface.

```sh
./axion talk --speaker examples/braid-reader.mind --listener examples/braid-witness.mind
./axion talk --speaker examples/braid-reader.mind --listener examples/braid-witness.mind < examples/braid-conversation.txt
```

These are actual answers from the second half of the recording, after opening a new tray, completing the pulls and covering the speaker’s view of the outcome.

```text
where did amber start?
speaker> north
and there now?
speaker asks> what is in north?
witness> teal
speaker> teal
where is teal?
speaker> north
where should amber be?
speaker> south
where would amber be without the last pull?
speaker> west
```

The two animals share eight grounded object names and ten learned question forms. The operator additionally knows the movements associated with `reed spin tuck turn`. The other body learned `loop lift dip roll` in a different workshop. A third worker can receive the interpreter’s rendering of the original request and act on its own differently arranged tray. In the supplied three-body exchange the speaker receives three words, the interpreter requests three marked views, then says `lift loop`. The worker moves from 2013 to 3021 in socket order. ## What was measured

The principal test starts with a previously untested motor sequence and a randomized vocabulary. A shutter hides the actual outcome from the operator. Only the last stroke is blocked in one of two otherwise identical histories. A second creature sees both public endpoints and misses the earlier conversation. The final acoustic prompt is identical across twins. Its intended subject comes from the preceding mention or reply.

## Work, dialects and selection

Four sockets and a finite dye inventory make it possible to replay every outcome exactly. Each body has its own motor registration. One pull can exchange a pair and transmit tension to another pair. The float-work organ has four motor and up to eight sound assemblies. Each assembly represents sixteen potential connections. A presented movement suppresses inconsistent routes according to the resident’s inherited truth tables. Capacity, selectivity, recurrent inhibition, word-order updates, motor depth and sensory projections can mutate. Raw sounds acquire a private receptive slot upon hearing. The somatic cortex still develops its own cell count during life and across births.

Compound demonstrations give the sound sequence and the visible endpoints. In one four-word selection strain, learners completed 891 of 1,024 novel five-word requests. Isolated-word demonstrations reached 32 endpoints at the same test. The focal operation had never been demonstrated alone under the compound treatment. In six-word communities the order of noncommuting operations can vary. An interpreter from another community can observe marked trials, choose a native phrase and send only its sound to the worker. Of 512 translated requests, 298 had a correct inferred relation, a physically correct endpoint and a public phrase whose meaning the independent auditor reconstructed correctly. Of those, 229 required a multiword translation. [Learning controls](braids/transfer.csv) · [Three-body replay](braids/translation.tsv.gz)

A different nursery lets founders coin sounds during their own movements. Juveniles attend to the calls and the displaced pigments. The apparatus can select parents by carbon returned at independently preset outlets. One such population supplied 9,317 successful spoken requests out of 12,288 after four generations and 9,345 after sixteen. Muting lessons produced zero at four generations. A nursery in which the founder’s own sounds helped define what counted as useful work often collapsed to synonyms. Its tested sixteen-generation population fulfilled zero independent filter requests. [Transmission by generation](braids/transmission.csv) · [Per-outlet outcomes](braids/cultures.tsv.gz)

The two `braid-call` individuals have the same germline and come from different counterbalanced histories of community 82. Both operated all 23 nonidentity outlet arrangements in the four-generation culture trial. They execute different movements when given the identical sound `@8` at the same initial arrangement and hand registration. This pair was chosen for contrasting productive dialects. ## The living watershed

Shifted two-by-two partitions let wired floats exchange places on a torus. Adjacent basins exchange carbon through woven apertures under a local pressure gradient. A resident can pull a carrier, work the fibre, emit a call, observe another body or eat the arriving cargo. Inputs from material movement and bank acoustics enter the actual somatic spike computation. Identical-state replays removing each input changed 26,858 and 1,201 sampled motor choices respectively. Those counts establish a causal effect on action. The actual population also made 5,573 less effective and 4,767 more effective reaches than its native fallback in the measured goal-directed comparisons. [Field histories and accounts](braids/watershed.csv)

Two neighbouring mineral ridges support each outlet. Their relief changes which two pigments align. Under positive flow, loose grains can lodge in the downstream bank. A sufficiently pressurized obstruction can release a grain. These exchanges use the existing conserved clay stock. Animals can also move that stock using their bodies. In the release, through-flow had lodged 447,437 grains and obstructed water had scoured 612,981. At 18,898 depositions a responding resident had moved the destination float. A same-state comparison with flat collars admitted a different amount of carbon at 4,642,984 contacts. Actual relief admitted 2,663,473 additional units and blocked 2,874,630. This establishes a lasting, manipulable change to flow. Deliberate hydraulic design by the wild creatures remains unverified. [Mobile, pinned and bypassed controls](braids/field-controls.csv)

## GPU representation and scope

The float organ uses 48 bytes per resident including its 16-byte germline. Each geographic carrier occupies two bytes. Pressure associations add four bytes per resident and an optional four-frame event record adds twelve. The released habitat uses 66 bytes per site for these arrays, plus small sharded counters. An acquired conversational memory adds 568 bytes only to a saved or housed speaker with such tissue. It holds raw acoustic identities, sparse question constructions, contact exclusions and discourse state. Inherited four-entry update tables determine which observations change those contacts. The event types, the compact candidate set, the request protocol and the physical conservation laws are architectural choices.

Matrix composition propagates four simultaneous source spikes through packed 16-bit rows. Planning searches at most 24 physical arrangements. A frozen motor lookup occupies 116 bytes per body. In a warmed 4,194,304-request benchmark, the compiled path took 1.781 ms and the live route search took 3.035 ms, with identical returned programs. Its lookup applied to 2,977,367 requests. In the untrained pool the live path was faster, 0.557 ms against 0.837 ms. Timings are medians of five device-event intervals and omit host transfers. The complete living watershed advanced its final 1,073,741,824 site-ticks in 24.619 s, or 43.62 million site-ticks/s, on the RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell laptop. The complete habitat state reported 1,981 bytes/site. [Planner measurements](braids/planner.csv) · [Whole-world timings](braids/watershed.csv)

These conversations are grounded in four floating objects, local actions and the last remembered event. A consistently untruthful witness can give a convincing false answer. The experimenter supplies the opportunity to ask, and the classroom supplies examples for question grammar. Spontaneous human language, general child-level reasoning and open-ended technological progress have yet to be demonstrated.

## Research behind the tests

- Macaques performing related tasks reused neural representations for components of the tasks. In August 2026, Osako and colleagues examined reusable operations in mice and recurrent networks. Axion uses a shared representation for a seen, requested or intended displacement, with a private mapping to each body’s muscles. This is an engineering hypothesis tested through transfer to new hands and vocabularies. [Primate study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12872450/) · [Mouse study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02410-0)
- Ruff and colleagues reported on November 10, 2026 that stimulation of two regions encoding motion and reward had different effects on monkeys’ decisions. The field audit therefore measures which motor choices change on withdrawal of a signal from the very same somatic state. [Stimulation study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02514-7)
- Human EEG during overlapping movie episodes linked shared representations to cross-event inference and differentiated representations to memory for individual events. The simulation stores a private episode alongside reusable learned transitions. This motivates testing a jam that distinguishes a remembered result from an otherwise plausible prediction. [Human episodic-memory study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68473-6)
- Nyhout and colleagues studied how parents and children aged three to six discuss changes to shared past events. A 2026 third-party learning study examined toddlers observing two adults. These studies motivated separate controls for a participant who missed an earlier turn, an observer who could see the action, and an instructor who supplies an example. [Parent–child counterfactuals](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41037444/) · [Third-party learning](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13646476/)

## Reproduce the experiments

Run from the repository root. Use a fresh output path for each evidence file.

```sh
make -j3 CXX=/usr/bin/g++ CUDA_CLANG=/usr/bin/clang++ CUDA_ARCH=sm_120 NATIVE=1
make test-braids CUDA_ARCH=sm_120 NATIVE=1
./axion floodplain --load examples/floatbank.flood --steps 0 --braid-seed 130243 --braid-inquiries 256 --braid-examples 48 --braid-holdout 2 --braid-report-trace events.tsv
./axion floodplain --load examples/voicebank.flood --steps 0 --braid-villages 256 --braid-generations 4 --braid-visits 48 --braid-seed 451537 --braid-songbook songs.tsv
python3 tools/braid-audit.py events.tsv songs.tsv
python3 tools/braid-audit.py docs/braids/*.tsv.gz
./axion talk --speaker examples/braid-reader.mind --listener examples/braid-witness.mind < examples/braid-translation.txt
./axion talk --speaker examples/braid-call-0.mind --listener examples/braid-call-1.mind < examples/braid-calls.txt
./axion floodplain --load examples/braids.flood --steps 1024 --save next.flood
```

For a fresh evolutionary experiment, start from `examples/resonator-bank.flood` with `--braid-epochs 128 --braid-parents 1024 --braid-selection food --braid-seed 34123`. A uniform-parent comparison accepts the same experiences and resources. The warm six-word lineage continued for 256 epochs with 1,536 candidates and variable listening order. At release the native Blackwell and portable CUDA paths produced byte-identical habitat states, minds and per-case transcripts in the regression fixtures. All 24 native regression targets passed.
