# Backwaters

Two residents working opposite ends of a strand can hear a knock and pull food out of the silt. The knock can come from a body, or from sediment and water deflecting an unattended strand. Neither pitch has an assigned meaning. A resident can eat at either of two banks and, under scarce access, solicit just one measurement before choosing.

## Where the food comes from

Four neighboring sites form a working district. The CUDA scheduler advances one quarter of the districts per tick and rotates which corner can initiate an encounter. A resident with feeding pores and limbs can spend energy to reach. A neighbor can spend its own energy to vibrate a membrane. Its private inputs are local load, water and a somatic motor spike. Mutable genes determine whether it emits a crest, a trough, or silence. A four-class timbre distinguishes the resonator. Several animals can share a timbre. A listener cannot inspect the remote animal’s memory or the carbon at the other end.

A taut fibre transmits impulses. Folding it or reversing the loading across an axis can invert an arriving deflection. A passive strand responds to the opposing pressure of the loaded end, the water and the retaining lip. The same weave increases a limb’s reach. Under silting, wet woven sites trap loose carbon in existing cavities. The bank resists displacement according to its cover and lip. An animal striking the other end can contribute force and intercept some of the loosened sediment. The food reaching each pore, the work charged to each body, and the carbon left in the ground are separately accounted for. The optional law adds no per-site material array. Existing organisms can build, fold or dismantle the strands on which it operates. [Physical implementation](../src/flood_rattles.cuh)

## A receipt the learner can actually obtain

The ear has 16 two-bit synapses, a bistable context, and one outstanding sensory trace. One circuit can use both banks of synapses in different contexts. Another can devote spare cells to forecasting whether its own earlier prediction and the taste pulse will agree. The alternatives compete for the same four bytes. The ear can use or alias the timbre and the pitch. Its germline also controls the state readout, sampling, inquiry preference, sensory delay, and two complete four-state transition tables. The offspring’s membranes and learning tables can mutate, and its private contacts start empty.

A choice exposes a single physical pan. The body swallows whatever it can recover there. Two to nine ticks later its gustatory trace becomes eligible to change a contact. One extension lets a pulse at the other end of a movement receive credit. For instance, hearing a neighbor and pulling **away** from it can put that neighbor’s pulse in the trace. Evolution controls whether this happens and how the actual taste changes the synapse. The learner receives no sample of what the unpulled pan would have yielded. Counterfactual observations used in the field report run outside its afferents. One complete organ occupies 24 bytes, including 16 bytes of germline. Independently growing somatic spiking tissue still generates the motor events that recruit it. [Ear and plasticity](../include/axion/rattle.hpp) · [Somatic development](neurogenesis.md)

## Why test a change of source

In a 2026 immersive foraging experiment, human groups tracked a moving resource more effectively under conditions where they could observe others’ payoffs and use them selectively. A separate 2026 set of human experiments found incidental learning of conflicting associations even across changing, weakly signaled contexts. Together they suggested an intervention for Axion. Preserve the current public evidence, change which earlier source had access to the resource, and ask whether the animal now behaves differently. [Foraging study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42778636/) · [Context-learning experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42666690/)

In a human single-neuron experiment published on November 4, 2026, brief experience with an intact visual object altered later neural responses to degraded views of it. The bank tests similarly hold a final sensory input constant while experimentally varying past experience. The result motivated the paired sensory test. The biological experiment measured cortical activity, while Axion measures the consequences of intervening on artificial contacts. Experiments in which people underweighted another participant’s information also motivated independent controls on receiving a report and learning from its consequence. [Human single-neuron recordings](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42982922/) · [Information from others across three experiments](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2026-09/Not_Learning_From_Others.pdf)

## The paired test

Two copies of each sampled germline start a life with the same empty private state. Each encounters two strikers. One touches a pan supplied by the working spring. The other touches a disconnected side pool. The living receiver can spend a reach on either of the two pans. Source positions alternate, different worlds carry different timbres and contact polarity, and the spring silently moves to the other striker for a second season before returning for a third.

Every held-out panel exhausts both binary loads and both striker positions. Four of eight problems need opposite reaches across the twins even though the two available signals are identical. With one permitted inquiry, the animal can change which of those two identical *available* sources it hears. All probe replies are read from independent copies of the post-exposure ear. No probe trains the next one. Silencing the reply, freezing the ear before or after the diversion, masking timbre, mistiming the taste, asking the opposite source, and requiring a random inquiry supply different controls. The independent auditor reconstructs the physical location of the stocked pan, the public impulses, the query and the motor readout from saved words. [Probe implementation](../src/rattle_trials.cuh) · [Auditor](../tools/bank-audit.py) · [Trace checksums](backwaters/audit.csv)

In the standardized nursery the two physical strikers have designed, discriminable transfer functions. A food-selected juvenile with a new opportunity history must learn the local convention. Uniform reproduction exposes every parent to the same physical choices. The food tournament uses carbon actually swallowed in that nursery. Adult germlines travel to the common garden, while their private weights stay behind.

With resident strikers, each group has three separately instantiated bodies. Starting germlines are random in the unseeded cohorts. A mutable body can emit a different polarity, use silence, or change behavior with its wetness or motor state. A gripper on the selected end can receive two of the six particles, leaving four for the puller. Each individual’s reproductive comparison uses its own returns as a puller and as a gripper for other animals. Districts with separate resonators give each source a distinguishable timbre. An additional test leaves the bodies’ own timbres intact. Reassigning germline parents after a round is an experimental nursery operation. The continuing inhabited watershed uses births, deaths and the actual energy held by its residents.

## Speaking to the listener

```sh
./axion talk --speaker examples/backwater-listener.mind --listener examples/runnel-consultant.mind < examples/backwater-conversation.txt
```

The bank listener carries hearing tissue drawn from a food-selected population and the acquired word maps of the earlier Runnels apprentice. The illustrative script uses the first of three numbered worlds tried in ascending order where this specimen changed its spoken source name after diversion and could cite a decisive impulse. Its accuracy is evaluated separately on held-out world sets. The worker has its own word maps. `/backwater SEED crossed` opens a two-pan apparatus. `choose` asks the speaker to pick a source. It names one with a spoken question. `/backwater-pass` lets the worker decode that name, feel the selected impulse, and describe it. A person can also supply a learned source name and its pitch word. The speaker can give a direction, answer `why`, or consider `without <source>`. A reason is voiced only when removing the heard impulse changes the decision at the original tie order. `/backwater-pull` spends energy on the chosen pan. `/backwater-work N` repeats these paid public exchanges. `/backwater-divert` changes the water without notifying the animals.

The host transcribes sounds. Both animals’ decoding, the selection of an interlocutor, the private updates, the spoken counterfactual and the physical reach execute on CUDA. The question forms, two-pan procedure, four timbres and binary impulses are designed. An evolved private sensorimotor convention and a supplied spoken vocabulary have different origins. The experiments measure discrimination, uptake and source revision within this setting. General conversation and child-level reasoning remain open. [Public word circuit](../src/siphon_backwater.cuh)

## The cost of investigating one source

With both standardized strikers available, three independently selected populations chose **22,342 of 24,576** stocked pans after the spring switched. Uniform reproduction gave 12,332. On the 6,144 paired conflicts, the selected groups were right in both worlds 5,044 times and the uniformly reproduced groups 233 times. These denominators count repeated measurements on 1,536 paired histories, across three evolutionary seeds. [All three seasons and channel controls](backwaters/common-gardens.csv)

Scarce access changed the problem. In an early experiment that credited only the pulse at the pan actually reached, the three selected cohorts recovered 13,053 of 24,576 post-diversion targets. In a second experiment, juveniles could evolve a connection between an earlier sound and the outcome of a reach to the opposite bank. They could also allocate spare cells to remembering whether their forecasts had agreed with later tastes. Three independently food-selected populations from fresh random founders addressed the connected source **22,290 times** and made **22,663 correct reaches**. A random-inquiry intervention on the same individuals made 17,758 correct reaches. Preventing both extensions in these same germlines at evaluation left 13,912. The two evolutionary experiments used different population sizes and generation counts. The interventions on the same animals isolate what their acquired circuits actually used. [Original per-choice records](backwaters/opposed-taste.tsv.gz) · [Family selection accounts](backwaters/selection.csv)

## When all three roles can evolve

The reward for a gripper was the carbon it caught. Evolution could increase the useful dependence of a body’s emissions on the load. Of three two-source populations started from random germlines, two developed useful acoustic codes and one remained near chance. In one successful population, 1,924 of 2,116 combinations of living body, wetness and motor parity produced a low impulse on empty sediment and a high impulse on loaded sediment. Another 185 combinations sounded only in one load condition. These are responses elicited from the recovered transducers. A body still needs the motility, opportunity and energy to strike in the field. [Expressed transducer responses](backwaters/strikers.csv) · [Native waveform evidence](backwaters/evolved-strikers.tsv.gz)

Three populations founded from the first successful two-source population then faced a single-inquiry district for 256 generations. The teachers and learners used their own mutable strikers, and generations turned over. These three populations made **20,245 of 24,576** correct reaches with one inquiry after diversion. Three populations starting directly under those conditions from random bodies made **12,085** correct reaches. The successful groups share an ancestral population. Longer unseeded runs and exchanges between different source populations are included in the evidence. [Single-inquiry records](backwaters/single-inquiry.tsv.gz) · [Held-out transfer and duration controls](backwaters/common-gardens.csv)

## How long can discovery take?

Keeping the population at 1,536 and holding the initial seeds, selection procedure and held-out worlds fixed produced three different histories. All three started with random membranes and strikers, in districts with one available inquiry. Seed 95603 progressed from 3,960 correct reaches out of 8,192 at generation 128 to 5,431 at generation 512. It fell to 5,208 at generation 768, reached 6,525 at generation 1,024 and 6,823 at 1,536, and ended at 6,488 at generation 2,048. The other two seeds finished at 3,879 and 3,994. ## What the wild residents did

The 512 × 512 watershed reached tick 302,464 with 75,323 living residents, 5,695 expressed body forms and 4,833,088 conducting somatic spiking cells. The new organs had been present for 49,152 ticks. Across that interval, the developmental ledger recorded 23,958,304 somatic cells added within lifetimes and 3,660,512 retired, alongside births and deaths. Of the 29,056,082 bank reaches, residents swallowed 15,649,665 units and grippers received a further 27,034. During the silted period, 29,840 reaches had opposing muscle tension. On 94 of them the puller’s own force was insufficient under the same load, and joint force released carbon. Across all joint reaches, the extra amount released relative to the puller acting alone was 4,993 units. The comparison uses local force, material and available mass at the moment of the pull. It demonstrates a mechanical affordance and its use. A shared plan between the two animals remains unverified. [Physical ledger](backwaters/watershed.csv)

The intact acoustic readout changed 3,066,630 immediate choices against an otherwise identical silent readout. Its net food difference was **+303,733**. The corresponding net effect of the listener’s acquired contacts was **−20,149**, and suppressing only resident emissions identified a net contribution of **−49,589** from those emissions. The passive-strand contribution was **+348,253**. These comparisons overlap. Each holds the current material, offers and tie order fixed, and measures a single choice. Whole-population fitness requires a separate comparison.
