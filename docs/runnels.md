# Runnels

A strand can act as a valve. A creature can fold it, flex it, watch a neighbor work it, and discover which way water passes under pressure. A row of such valves can move water and dissolved food uphill when its chambers expand and contract. In the inhabited watershed a resident pays for handling fibre and can eat carbon arriving at its pores. Both the work and the resulting structure can outlive the builder.

## A conversation you can replay

The apprentice has experience with three pairs of mineral faces. The consultant has handled a fourth. Each animal begins with its own working memory and previously learned acoustic names. The instructor used just the reference surfaces. Neither requested joint occurred during instruction. The two stored individuals combine folding tissue sampled from separate evolving populations with speech-capable watershed bodies. Their vocabularies and the grammar for this task were supplied. The first order is *choose amber ivory brook*. The apprentice initially asks *why slate ivory brook*. The consultant has no usable answer, and the apprentice tries *why ivory ivory brook*. The consultant replies *turn ivory ivory pool face pool*. This describes a fold on the reference joint and predicts its deflection. The apprentice then says *turn amber ivory rill*. It asks the worker to make a **different movement on different material**. Asked *why*, it names the intermediate ivory–amber joint. After the consultant’s folding memory is cleared, the apprentice instructs *turn slate ivory mere* for the second joint. The worker can still understand the instruction and move its hand. Running the bellows delivers source carbon through the two folds. [Spoken orders](runnels/spoken-orders.csv)

In an interactive session, `/flume WORLD SPAN handed` opens a chiral apparatus. `/flume-order` voices the next request. A `choose` utterance can request a new material pair or direction. `/flume-pass` delivers the current public message or executes the heard instruction. `why` asks the speaker to identify its reference. `face LEFT RIGHT ANGLE` asks about an experienced fold. `/flume-build` lets the two residents work through the gallery unaided. `/flume-pump TICKS` runs the resulting device. The physical collector and consumed carbon persist between pressure cycles. Samples on separate coupons spend two units from a finite tuition reserve.

## What the tissue learns

Each material has a surface groove and a spring normal. A strip seated between two surfaces responds to the difference between their normals and the angle at which it was folded. It may yield in one direction, both directions, or neither. A handed surface can reflect a movement. Rotating the installer’s native wrist changes the local motor address for the same public gesture. Local saturation, mineral mass, banks, water, dissolved carbon and remaining fibre still constrain transport. The pressure cycle supplies external mechanical work. Its energy is prescribed. Water, carbon and bodily work have independent accounts.

The inhabited watershed executes each resident’s somatic spiking brain and its folding tissue. The controlled nurseries and material probes isolate folding circuits sampled from resident germlines. The optional folding tissue uses 12 bytes of germline, 32 bytes of private binary contacts, and four bytes of experience counts per resident. A pair of sensory spikes identifies a groove combination. A motor spike selects one of four gestures. Two sets of contacts predict the opposed flex responses. Separate inherited four-entry Boolean tables govern writing from self-experience and from observation. Depending on the genome, a subsequent event can preserve, reverse, erase or replace a contact. Offspring inherit mutable genes and start with empty private folding contacts. The existing developmental system independently grows and retires somatic spiking cells. [Somatic development](neurogenesis.md)

The learner can also compare the responses of two hands on a common reference surface. It admits a rotation or reflection when it is the only compatible correspondence with sufficient observed overlap. It can then project the response to a different surface, and conflicting projections can suppress a choice. The optional pathway, sensory selectivity, minimum overlap, eligibility of reflections and plasticity tables are heritable. The correspondence circuit and its candidate symmetry groups are engineered. Neither the queried material’s hidden normal nor a physics simulator is passed into this inference. During conversation the pupil can ask about a missing reference. It hears a proposal in public coordinates and incorporates the reported consequence through its own sensory circuit. ## Experiments behind the design

A human and macaque experiment found that participants combined observations of another player with their own outcomes, with differences between the evidence sources. A mouse experiment found flexible use of an observed action–outcome relationship after the apparatus changed. These findings motivated independent afferents for private and witnessed flexes, remappable hands, and controls that preserve the physical example while disrupting its relation to the observer. [Evidence accumulation from experience and observation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12931446/) · [Observational learning and flexible action](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02292-2)

In a human study published on 16 November 2026, participants used a learned relational structure to infer connections. Perturbing hippocampal activity impaired structure-based inference. Axion’s analogous test withholds the target, removes one reference at a time, and independently checks the physical correspondence cited for each answer. A separate human experiment showed how technology can improve through transmission under conditions with limited causal information. In Axion, successfully delivering carbon, citing a reference, and giving a physically faithful explanation are therefore three separate measurements. [Human relational structure and inference](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43040044/) · [Transmission and causal reasoning in technological change](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgag090)

Longitudinal work on orangutans distinguished watching skilled work from merely being near a worker and documented interactions between observation and practice. The waterway assays allow successive independent operators to observe, practice and inherit a changed object. The controls independently hide work, remove folds at turnover, or retain one adult on the same bank. [Observation during nest building](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08217-2) · [Social and individual learning across development](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adz6415)

## An unworked joint in two indistinguishable apparatuses

Each calibrated material pair admits exactly one strictly correct direction-specific fold. In the paired tests the target looks identical across worlds, and the correct motor differs. The juvenile encounters three reference joints. It has never touched the target. The independent auditor enumerates all four gestures, checks both calibrations, reconstructs the two-chamber pump, and verifies each reported rotation or reflection against the four actual motor responses.

Three rotary-material cohorts selected for source carbon solved **5,818 of 6,144** pairs in both worlds. Direct lookup in the same cohorts solved 10. Uniform reproduction with the correspondence circuit available solved 95. The original tally counted whether the creature used the intended reference corner. A further audit exposed a distinction. For one population trained with fixed assignments of self and peer experience and tested on chiral material, 1,902 of 2,048 pairs were solved, but only **921** had physically correct complete motor correspondences in both worlds. A private code could support a good choice without faithfully describing all four physical motions. ## Can one speaker find what another one knows?

The speaker experiences three surfaces in one material column. The consultant sees only one joint in a different column. Their private contacts are separate. Choosing the requested target as the question leaves the consultant without the required information. A successful indirect question names a different mineral pair. After hearing the report, the pupil must translate the proposed fold into its own hand, use a relation across surfaces to produce another instruction, and justify the result. A final assay changes the target to another untouched surface and mutes consultation. Only the first-stage evidence can carry over.

The first pair solved all 512 rotary pairs and 255 of 512 handed pairs. Two further individuals came from the varied-source populations. With the reflection-sensitive individual as speaker, they solved **1,024 of 1,024** paired requests in the handed world and **2,048 of 2,048** individual follow-up requests without another report. In 1,514 follow-ups the second successful movement differed from the first. Asking only about the requested target, muting the consultant, or falsifying its fold each reduced paired first-stage success to zero. Reversing these two animals’ roles yielded 513 of 1,024 paired first-stage successes in handed material. These measurements include independent waveform decoding, physical work and pressure replay. The exposed vocabulary is small and the questions concern a specific mechanism. ## How much time does a construction need?

In a controlled 64-joint strip the experimenter supplied aligned valves. The regular pressure cycle transported 720 units of source carbon from an inlet head of 5 to a collector head of 17. Reversing all valves, cutting the middle, unfolding the strips, or stopping the pressure cycle each left zero source carbon at the collector. The water initially downstream of a cut can still move. [Controlled physical accounts](runnels/controlled-physics.csv)

A second assay gave one district experience on even material types and another on odd types. Their graduates visibly bent and tested real joints. A new pupil could watch both and use its own private attempts before assembling a new gallery. With 24 offered encounters per district, ten independent practice attempts per generation and selective attention, **357 of 512** held-out 96-joint galleries supplied source carbon using relational readout. The direct-readout group used identical opportunity budgets and supplied 274 collectors. All 357 successful combined pupils produced carbon where both immediate predecessors failed separately on the same 96-joint gallery. In an early 192-community trial, inexperienced demonstrators induced 39,792 contact edits from 67,584 observed gestures, compared with 32,040 edits from the same number of gestures by experienced adults. The corresponding pupils delivered 14,563 and 10,225 carbon. The generational results depend on the amount and type of experience. [Gallery and exposure sweeps](runnels/transmission.csv)

A third assay starts with an entirely unfolded channel. An operator can walk, bend a strip, test the current one, or consume carbon at the outlet. It has only local observations and a finite energy purse. The inherited motor table reads the presence of near and far carbon and a built fold. The next juvenile retains what it actually saw. Food emptied from the collector enters that individual's purse. Selection of keeper germlines used food actually swallowed across two other systems of two to five joints.

Across three selected populations, **all 1,536 eight-joint communities** delivered source carbon in eight generations of 48 actions per operator. They delivered 392,666 units in total. Keeping the structures and closing the view gave 384,369. Flattening each inherited structure gave 123,674. Removing both the structures and vision gave 79,349. Uniform reproduction with both forms of inheritance available supplied 5,998 units across 248 successful communities. Much of the selective gain came from the built environment itself. [Paid work, consumption and turnover](runnels/maintenance.csv)

Longer channels exposed the cost of starting over. With 96 joints, none of 256 communities delivered within 12 generations of 96 actions. With 192 actions per operator, successful communities rose from zero of 128 at 12 generations to 34 at 24 and 53 at 48. The last group delivered **81,132** units. Flattening the work at every birth yielded zero in those 48-generation trials. Closing only the line of sight actually increased delivery to 97,347 units, and keeping a single adult at work delivered 357,586. The illustrated history follows the first productive community in index order. Its collector first contains visible carbon in the snapshot at generation 29. [Six physical snapshots of the 96-joint channel](../journal/runs/00009615-floodplain/plate.webp)

This demonstrates cumulative physical construction and a strong cost of turnover. It gives no evidence of universally beneficial teaching. [Maintenance length and time sweeps](runnels/maintenance.csv)

Removal turned 408 successful choices into errors and corrected 417 errors. Ordinary residents could still act and the nonlinear habitats diverged after intervention. In reciprocal competitions the lineage allowed to observe finished close to half the population. ## CUDA execution and reproducibility

Each growing site uses one optional byte for its two folds. Spare bits store attribution. An additional 48 bytes per resident hold the plastic folding apparatus, including its genome. Counters are sharded. Folding runs inside the existing resident-contact kernel, and fluid transport inside the water kernel. Enabling the mechanics adds no kernel launch to a normal watershed tick. A soil transplant retains the public folds and discards population-specific social attribution. The recipient’s acquired motor contacts stay with its body. A warp can compile one learned policy with one lane per texture and requested direction. Its ballot stores the 32 admissibility decisions and two 32-bit words store the motors. The 12-byte image is specific to that circuit, tie order and available tissue.

On the Blackwell laptop, 529 practised circuits occupied 6,348 action-image bytes. They had 4,072 inferred entries. Over 1,048,576 queries, direct resolution took 1.056 ms, compilation took 0.0196 ms, and packed dispatch took 0.0128 ms. All chosen actions and admissibility bits agreed. These are device-event times for a stable collection. Whole-watershed advancement, classroom work, image compilation, and image dispatch have separate timing scopes in the journal.
