# A question across the current

Two animals can share the food released by a physical filter. One animal touches the inlet and the other touches the outlet. Each has information the other needs. A call can direct attention to one of four properties and a second call can guide the downstream grip. Both sound-to-perception mappings are acquired during life. The outlet retains its new position when both animals leave. Subsequent water can passively free carbon for other organisms.

## What suggested this experiment

Valet and colleagues recorded a socially maintained signal convention in 91 chimpanzees spanning eight groups. Stable use depended on the presence of experienced role models. Field recordings of common marmosets also found that the identity of an initial call predicted aspects of the response. Those results motivated two engineering decisions here. Each participant can choose whether and how to vocalize, and a third individual can hear the encounter while observing its consequences. [Chimpanzee signal conventions, published online November 9, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2606837123) · [Wild marmoset vocal exchanges](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42393355/)

Human experiments in a novel signalling medium found that people could use deviations from expected movements to communicate. In neural recordings, short visual learning experiences also changed how people represented degraded images. The proposed simplification in Axion is a small, writable mapping between local perceptions, movements and sounds whose expression depends on experience. The choice of a two-bit lookup table is an engineering assumption. [Human communication through motion](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-57025-z) · [Human single-neuron recordings during rapid visual learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02496-6)

Work on artificial social learners has used interchangeability of roles and transmission between agents to analyze an acquired code. Axion uses role exchange, shuttered channels, changes to a single unseen property, independently reared copies and cross-fostering as interventions. [Emergent communication in social learning agents](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51887-5)

## The physical substrate

The first two material properties are the grain's light-absorption band and the coating expressed by its catalyst in local solvent. The other two come from the forward and backward grain faces. Exposed soil supplies alternative optical and contact properties at a bare inlet. The seed, root and composition at the outlet select its responsive band. A rotating slit passes finite bound carbon or detritus into edible fruit when the physical alignment fits. Water on both sides powers a joint contact. Muscular work consumes energy. Feeding requires a pore and a grip requires a limb. The existing budget includes work as heat, uneaten fruit and all remaining organic stock.

Four independently adjustable faces occupy one byte per site. Flow through a suitable face sometimes moves organic stock even with nobody present. Twisting, shifting and sealing those faces therefore changes a material stock, including outside the interaction that produced the setting. The earlier mineral, sediment, construction, solvent and spiking-growth systems continue to operate on this ground. A discrete local lattice and disjoint pair updates allow all water transport, material transformations, body movement, perception, plasticity, birth and death to run on CUDA. [Reactive mineral groups and hydraulic structures](materials.md) · [Evolvable spiking populations](neurogenesis.md)

The optional acoustic field adds eight bytes per site. A local contact can leave a ringing material and a visible after-stroke mark. Clay geometry, organic fibres and the outcome set the duration, up to 512 ticks. The wave and mark carry the emitted form and local physical appearance. An unrelated animal needs a receptive expression gate and optical access to associate them. A tag excludes the two direct participants from the third-party count. Material impressions survive a soil transplant for their remaining lifetime. A wash damps them immediately.

## What evolves

Each resident has independently heritable contact, reinforcement, release, lateral, anatomy and motor-expression words. Their 192 bits can mutate and recombine. Three writable four-by-four projections bind perception to vocal address, perception to vocal report, and proprioception to movement. Each contact has four possible states. A gene chooses the next two-bit state from its old state, local activity and the outcome. Other genes choose release levels, competition between receptors, resting preferences, sensory wiring, responses to visual access, willingness to inquire, reply or grip, and willingness to overhear. Sounds and mappings can coincide or stay silent. The somatic network drives the organ and receives its resulting volley. The general growth and body systems have separate heritable architectures.

Evolution operates within explicit physical and representational bounds. There are four possible sounds, two ordered opportunities to vocalize per encounter and three discrete projections. The optional graded developmental prior biases founder transition tables toward related responses. The default does not install a named sound, a successful grasp mapping or an inherited memory. The chamber presents a fixed two-party task. The open watershed also offers grazing, movement, reproduction, competition and other construction opportunities, so using the filter is optional.

## Does a call carry private information?

In the first pilot, 214,336 audible calls accumulated. Under four substitutions of one hidden inlet property, none of the 16,384 tested quartets changed the downstream grip. A high call count alone proved uninformative. In the later habitat, the current intermittently cleared before a grip and the resonant material could carry a sound during movement. Newborns could calibrate their own hands and benefit from a neighbor while their code developed.

The released habitat starts from Springtide. A resource-funded nursery raised 8,192 pairs per season for 2,048 seasons. The nursery ranks reproductive access by affordable eggs from paid contacts. New watershed residents received its germlines with empty native contact fields. Their descendants then lived for 4,096 ordinary watershed ticks. An all-pairs screen found the following distribution.

Seven of the 555 screened pairs reached 75%. Slot 452 and slot 2518 were selected on seed 75173, then tested on independent seed 982163. They solved all four substitutions in 14,340 of 16,384 fresh quartets. The answer-shuttered pair solved zero complete quartets. Four further founder seeds compared proportional reproduction with a condition that distributes parenthood uniformly among adults that can afford eggs. Each used 1,024 seasons and 4,096 pair slots. After 384 shared practice opportunities, pairs in the proportional condition achieved 109,445 of 131,072 opaque trials. Shuttering the reply yielded 32,020 successes. Exchanging a receiver for the same genotype reared on another interaction history yielded 33,761. Uniform-parentage controls achieved 22,401 overall, with 22,400 when replies were shuttered. The raw tables retain abstentions and complete-quartet counts for each seed. [All transfer arms](siphons/siphon_transfer.csv)

Performance fell again under unrestricted dispersal and reproduction in these four smaller valleys. Only five of 3,667 neighboring pairs from the proportionally reared introductions reached 75%. Fresh, unselected valleys had no such pair among 3,510 neighbors. Their entire hearing-enabled population achieved only one more productive grip than the matched shuttered population over 898,560 challenges. Whether cooperation will spread depends on ecology and the histories partners share.

## What happened over another 65,536 ticks?

Four continuations began from the same occupied habitat. Two kept the original 64 × 64 area. Two opened an unoccupied 128 × 128 frontier after 2,048 additional ticks. Each geometry had a natural and a shuttered native channel. All four then ran to tick 95,616 under their own births, deaths, resources and somatic mutations.

Both enclosed populations became extinct. On the expanded ground the open channel ended with 4,265 living residents, 429 distinct bodies and generation 1,237. The shuttered counterpart ended with 4,442 residents and 235 bodies. The result comes from one matched founding habitat. It does not establish a population advantage for communication. Among all 3,940 closing open-channel neighbors, none reached 75% in the detached filter test. They produced 214,479 harvests in 1,008,640 opportunities, falling to 201,332 with their answer path closed. The late open population expressed 276,544 somatic cells, about 65 per resident under the 512-cell capacity. The late descendants are available as a separate habitat. ## Can others acquire it?

In cross-fostering, an unrelated newborn calibrates its own hand against an empty slit. It can then witness the actual utterances and physical consequences of an adult pair. The temporal control rotates utterances among visible movements in groups of eight encounters while preserving their frequencies. The shutter control preserves the visual sequence. No learned contact table passes from parent to child.

With fixed adult mappings, the first generation in the four selected nurseries harvested 106,291 times in 131,072 trials. Muting the demonstrations yielded 31,968 harvests and temporal reordering gave 31,197. Using adults straight from the released watershed with zero preparatory practice produced 49,613 harvests, versus 33,130 after muting. A second cohort in that experiment produced 61,611 versus 32,752. [Cross-fostering results by seed and cohort](siphons/siphon_foster.csv)

Environmental contact without a living demonstrator was weaker. Four fresh populations inherited the ground from Reedbed, including the decaying impressions. Native pair work was disabled for 16 ticks. Hatchlings heard 42,270 pre-birth impressions and changed 15,494 contact bits. Damping the field eliminated both counts. The hatchlings still achieved exactly one productive grip per four attempts in the private-source test. The surviving traces changed tissue, but the experiment supplies no evidence that they alone taught the cooperative skill. [Young ears and altered contacts](siphons/siphon_audience.csv)

## Words from shared events

The selected outlet animal received 96 human captions for visible after-stroke contacts. The teacher used two word orders, `from <property> came <appearance>` and `<appearance> beyond <property>`. Twelve of the sixteen property–appearance combinations were available for captioning. The four diagonal combinations were reserved for testing. The program receives a sequence of sound patterns and two coactive receptive events. It can learn up to 32 external words in this organ, with 48 remembered examples and eight positions per utterance. On retrieval, uniquely grounded sensory words can vary across examples. Other positions retain their witnessed forms. The punctuation token `?` requests one missing constituent.

For a missing appearance the resident queries a material channel and attempts a real grip. For a missing property it can ask about all four channels, looking for a unique match. Both modes require a return sound. The external controller checks complete transcribed answers after CUDA inference against the physical scene it originally set. The wavelength names, English strings and tested word orders live in the teaching script. A separately bred local word-learning rule was installed before captioning. With the original watershed lexical rule the outlet accepted the captions but gave zero correct unique completions on its control corpus. This distinction matters for interpreting the origin of the result.

The public corpus varies the private material, hole direction, order, aperture and control channel. A second taught vocabulary uses arbitrary forms and an independent lesson schedule. Freshly presented unique cases include arrangements absent from teaching. Other scenes contain repeated or absent values and cannot support a unique reverse answer. The explicit schema induction, bounded acoustic organ and fixed chamber procedure constrain the scope of this result. General conversation, unconstrained grammar acquisition, independently invented tools and child-level cognition remain open goals.

## Can an animal's construction outlast it?

A detached test preserves final water, soluble components, reactor grains, soils and carbon stocks, removes the animals and advances the ordinary physical kernels. A separately recorded snapshot supplies the original 16,384 outlet faces before the introduction. Occupation changed 7,878 of them. With the occupied settings, the current passively released 64 carbon units over 128 ticks. Restoring the original settings released 40 and changed the final fruit at 21 sites. Sealing all pores produced zero passive release. This isolates a persistent consequence of the animals' work. Whether the animals anticipated the later change in flow is a separate behavioral question. [All six matched physical landscapes](siphons/abandoned-filters.csv)

## What runs on the GPU

CUDA executes the physical world, spiking populations, native exchanges, local contact transitions, nursery selection, foster demonstrations, text grounding and counterfactual tests. The host presents external text as sound patterns, advances batches, transcribes responses, writes checkpoints and renders the journal. The two residents’ own local circuits supply each response.

The live organ costs 56 bytes per resident slot. It includes 24 bytes of heritable rules, 12 bytes of writable contacts, four lifetime counters and the latest volley. When plasticity is suspended, five four-by-four winner masks, sensory and joint wiring, three motor gate bytes and folded resting biases preserve the entire native decision in **18 bytes per animal**. The ordinary transfer assay uses this representation once per pair to test many scenes. The inhabited watershed retains writable circuits. Growth of the somatic spiking population remains independently heritable.

On the RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell laptop GPU, eight interleaved batches of 1,048,576 two-animal contacts produced 8,388,608 identical compact and writable traces. The median of seven timed batches was 0.098272 ms compact and 0.216672 ms writable. The resident table contained 529 animals and fits in cache. Each thread generated a scene and two resident indices, computed both utterances and the grip, and wrote an eight-byte neural and physical outcome. The 10.67 billion contacts/s figure applies to this batch. The entire 64 × 64 Reedbed ecology advanced at 60.75 million site-ticks/s in its recorded founding run. Compiling the habitat and acoustic kernels directly for Blackwell and forward-JIT compiling them from SM 80 PTX also produced identical expanded habitat bytes and 38 matching intervention and population rows after 91 ticks on the same GPU. In a live `axion talk` session, `/reed SEED [PRIVATE_SPECTRUM LOCAL_FILTER OPENING]` places a fresh table with finite stock. Each successive pair of bits in the spectrum is one physical channel. The seed fixes posture and tie-breaking. Enter `/duet` to watch the animals act. If a contact exposes an outcome, `/caption WORDS` provides a public utterance. `/reed-learning fixed` holds the native connections steady during external tuition, while energy, audible contacts and external word learning continue. `/reed-wire` accepts `open`, `query-shut`, `answer-shut`, `query-shift` and `answer-shift`. `/private-facts 4096 982163` runs the held pair controls on detached copies. `/leave-reed` closes the table. The habitat file retains owner-bound vocabulary across a release and export, and a newborn starts with empty acquired contacts.

The population cards group residents by the observed segment, eye, limb and pore layout. Each plate can show eight common forms. The voice field beside a portrait belongs to the oldest measured member of that form. A lit square means that a local vocal cell wins or ties for a particular material receptor. Adjacent cells may tie, and the native genes can gate emission entirely. The journey retains the dates and denominators of the new tests.
