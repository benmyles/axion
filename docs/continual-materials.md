## Twelve bytes of memory

Six binary afferents describe local pressure and material conditions. An additional 64-bit mask marks which of the 64 possible volleys this animal has passively attended. Three packed bytes can retain the stimulus and signed outcome of recent, self-generated diagnostic contacts. The fourth byte counts admitted observations since the last informative test. That is 12 additional bytes per equipped slot. All sensory writes pass through the animal's inherited, expressed spiking gate.

The readout fits the smallest compatible sets of afferents to the shared observational evidence. When two such models forecast opposite outcomes for a physically accessible stimulus, a signed personal observation can distinguish them. Recent observations have priority when the environment contradicts an old one. The animal’s inherited motor weights and interval help determine when it will revisit a disputed stimulus and what it can afford to manipulate. Pooled contacts still supply a forecast when this readout has insufficient evidence. There are no mineral identity labels or switch notifications among the animal's inputs. [State, retrieval and action choice](../include/axion/material_context.hpp) · [Paid sensory exposure](../include/axion/context_exposure.hpp)

The physical laws, choice of sensory afferents available to the body, candidate-model search, packing format and recency ordering are engineered. Receptor access, capacity, local Boolean update, somatic release, retention span and interest in checking vary by genome. For the two showcased guides the low four bits of the pressure update both encode OR, which can retain incompatible outcomes. Their full inherited words differ. The earlier population analysis counted these two quantities separately after a correction to its chart. ## Continuous, held-out tests

Each subject lived through 16 episodes without a memory reset. The coastal test used 384 separately seeded histories, each with the same animal’s inherited circuits and vocabulary. At each episode the guide could pay for 32 passive views and up to eight interventions before committing to a scarce-stock channel. The two substrates give identical passive observations but can require incompatible actions. The independent release kernel computed all 64 local physical responses and tested whether the construction worked. A full-law success requires correct forecasts for every local input. At the brook, 16,384 more ticks produced 3,893 physically diagnostic checks, 3,852 admitted updates and 15,547 decisions with a resolved context. Recent evidence changed 5,449 grip decisions and 213 net decisions across 3,881,514 awake comparisons.
