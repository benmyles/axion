# The millrace

A creature can spend its own energy to turn a mineral, fit a fibre loop between two neighbors, or reclaim fibre and leave a different mechanism behind. The direction a neighbor moves depends on the driving material, the form of the joint and, for a notched belt, the face touching it. Sometimes turning an accessible part is the only way to move a covered one. Sometimes the requested configuration is physically impossible until the creature changes a connection.

## A procedure you can hear and use

```sh
./axion talk --speaker examples/rack-guide.mind --listener examples/causeway-guide.mind < examples/rack-instructions.txt
```

The example starts with an animal carrying heritable material-learning tissue and a learned public vocabulary. The speaker observes work on separate elementary fixtures. You can present a new arrangement, ask for the current face of one piece, give a different target, ask which handle is necessary, or give the speaker’s instruction to an independent animal. In this recorded exchange the two bodies have different local actuator addresses.

```text
work order> choose ivory rill slate mere flint brook amber rill
animal> go amber brook
animal> go flint amber amber brook amber pool amber pool
animal> amber
```

The first answer addresses a partial request. The longer answer addresses the amended request. The pair *flint amber* names the two parts to connect with the available loop. *Amber brook* identifies the marked handle for the first stroke. The final *amber* answers *why*. Taking away that part’s handles makes the request unreachable in this fixture. Sending the procedure across the intact acoustic channel lets the recipient perform the work and receive carbon. The trace file records the actual public sounds as well as the recipient’s local and world motor addresses. ## What the research changed

Children in two populations learned concise procedures for generating patterns from examples. That motivated withholding the requested four-piece assemblies from selection and testing actual completion on new combinations. The study of children used visual sequences. Our test uses material interventions and different controls. [Children use algorithm induction to discover patterns in data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73029-9)

Macaques trained to draw could use familiar actions in novel compositions. The authors reported separable representations of actions in frontal cortex. We split public action descriptions from the specific actuator address in a given body and tested whether a second animal could remap them. [Neural representation of action symbols in primate frontal cortex](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10297-x)

Five of fifteen bumblebees that observed trained conspecifics subsequently opened a two-step puzzle alone. That result motivated charging the demonstrator for its work, removing it before the test, and measuring the effects of hiding the intermediate actions. Our three-generation assay measures how much behavior survives repeated teaching. [Bumblebees socially learn behaviour too complex to innovate alone](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07126-4)

## What changes during a lifetime

One heritable word encodes the local synaptic transition law and discharge threshold. A second word can express a different law for coupling and fitting. Two more govern recruitment, capacity, receptive gates, exploration, motor order, expression of selective attention and the depth of forward activation. Genetic mutation acts on individual bits of all four words. Offspring inherit the genes and start without the parent’s material memories.

A two-bit synapse updates after the before–action–after contact. Learning the effect of an impulse on unconnected material allows the recipient to infer which impulse, if any, caused a *different* piece to move across a seam. It can also learn how fitting changes the visible connection. Heritable self and witness gates can close independently. One to four private material contexts occupy a reusable, fixed-stride arena. The older somatic growth system continues to grow and retire actual spiking cells and transmit variable natal cell counts. [Somatic growth and its resource tests](neurogenesis.md)

At inference, discharged contacts propose material transitions. A bounded wavefront can visit at most 256 configurations of four four-faced pieces and emit up to 12 strokes. The construction circuit compares keeping the rig and adding one available connector. It ranks physically predicted outcomes by work, with one unit per stroke and two per attachment. Programs, placements, taught actions and observed effects vary by individual and world. The wavefront, chemical and mechanical primitives, utterance frames, action costs and maximum fixture size are engineered. These assumptions bound what this experiment can demonstrate.

One heritable attention gate admits a narrower search. A passively driven piece cannot itself transmit a second impulse during the same stroke. A resident may therefore ignore a bystander’s unknown response when it also disables that bystander’s handles in its own search. On 1,536 unfamiliar four-piece racks with unstudied peripheral parts, requiring the entire transition to be known produced 334 successes. Allowing the inherited gate produced 1,092. Expressing it in every subject produced 1,523, with zero incorrect proposals. Asking for every face reduced forced-focal success to 182. ## Learning to change an acquired rule

We tested whether the same private synapses could acquire a material law, respond when it changed and recover their earlier skill when it changed back. An October study also found that practicing motor abstractions can improve movement on new patterns. ## Blackwell cost and scale

A private mechanism mind costs 24 bytes plus 32 bytes per allocated context. The field adds a 20-byte provenance record per four sites and one small counter block per shard. The 16 bytes of genes are included in the 24-byte mind. The action image uses four bits per starting state for a fixed mechanism, reachable handles, goal, action order and depth. Three-piece and four-piece images occupy 32 and 128 bytes respectively, alongside the learned 64-byte transition chart and fixture description.

On the laptop RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell, one warp compiles each chart. Lanes exchange reachable-state masks with ballots and pack neighboring actions with a shuffle. Direct and compiled execution agreed across 6,291,456 full programs and refusals per benchmark population. For 512 practiced four-piece fixtures, 82,704 of 131,072 possible starting states needed a nonempty procedure. Median device-event times for 1,048,576 queries were 52.949 ms writable and 0.323 ms compiled. Compilation of all 512 images took 0.082 ms. With 579 actual wild fixtures, just 29 of 148,224 states could emit a nonempty plan. That group’s faster readout mostly reflects refusals. These isolated fixed-goal measurements exclude learning and world physics. The 4,096-tick active watershed segment ran at 44.04 million complete site-ticks per second.
