# What to tell a particular listener

Two resident spiking creatures can exchange information about an inlet that only one of them saw. The guide can choose which observation to offer or stay quiet. The pupil can learn how that guide's words correspond to movements in its own view. These abilities are measurable separately. Choosing an unfamiliar inlet sometimes made a listener's forecast worse until the pair had watched water together. ## What each animal can observe

The physical habitat already allows a limb to disturb one of two submerged grains. A nearby animal can open its eye and watch the result. The new connection lets a co-observer perceive that exposed aperture alongside its own view of the moving grain. Two visible outline features distinguish four classes. One of the two adjacent sites is sampled according to the observer's heading. Members of the same outline class can be confused. Sixteen contacts in the upper half of an existing somatic gate word hold four retinotopic traces for each of four outlines. Two mutable hearing loci govern their update, recurrent response, outlet choice and activation threshold. The tissue also participates in ordinary cortical activity. At the table, two visibly marked learners face the stream at different times. Each held-out pair forks the same learner's checkpoint into the two roles. The guide sees the public apertures and its own water images. Its inputs at decision time are the returning coat, its previous contacts, the two visible onsets and its own forecast activity. Every pair keeps the guide's previous water, cortical firing, opportunities and tie breaker identical. Which recipient returns can change the selected source. The apparatus can let pupils watch both sources, one or neither. An additional treatment lets a learner privately see the opposite stream while displaying its usual public movement. [Encounter and material release](../include/axion/audience_course.hpp) · [Independent replay](../tools/audience-audit.py)

The same muscles can move a real tracer or initiate a short report. For a report, the guide retrieves a name for the inlet and, when its own forecast is definite, a name for the expected outlet. A heritable connection determines their temporal order. The listener initially interprets the two sounds using its own inherited order and previously grounded nouns. Its own visual learning circuit receives the resulting activity. It can later learn a different interpretation for a familiar voice. The two positions in a report, the four possible directions and the physical costs are engineering choices. The weights, Boolean response tables, learned public names and decisions vary across animals.

A family received two finite portions and an allowance for the eyes, hand and voice. The food that reached its chosen outlets and any unused allowance could fund offspring at eight units each. ## Learning what a voice means

During a lesson, the guide watches a fresh arrangement, makes its own choice and speaks. When its limb is engaged, it also moves one supplied grain. The listener can hear two names and see the resulting motion. Four local 4-by-4 synaptic matrices associate either sound position with either temporal phase. The listener's inherited lexical plasticity acts on the admitted images. A row with contradictory evidence can close a temporal interpretation. The receiver uses a position once the surviving alternatives agree. Two existing 32-bit ear words hold the matrices. The resident's existing four-acquaintance memory associates them with an audible timbre. Older contacts can be displaced or altered by further experience. ## The cost of unnecessary speech

A second set of populations experienced all sixteen combinations of the two listeners' access to the two inlets. Paying for redundant speech could leave a family with less energy for offspring. In a nursery with six energy units per food portion, one selected circuit spoke on all 1,024 encounters with an entirely unexposed pupil and on zero of 1,024 with a fully exposed one. Another spoke on 954 and zero. A third spoke on 1,024 and 244. Several more generously fed guides spoke to both groups. These are differences among evolved circuits, with a best-performing specimen chosen for further tests. [Every scarcity treatment](audience/speaking-cost.csv) · [Selected circuit](../examples/audience-scarce.germ)

On three fresh seeds the selected guide made **1,536 offers to unexposed pupils and zero to fully exposed pupils**. Swapping the recipient's public input redirected all 1,536 offers to the fully exposed pupils. It emitted 3,072 sounds under either assignment. Correct forecasts rose from 3,072 to 4,608 with the intact assignment and stayed at 3,072 with the exchanged assignment. A hidden periscope that reversed the pupil's actual experience also removed the benefit. The behavior tracks public evidence of exposure. These experiments leave inference about a partner's private beliefs open. ## Natural selection in the watershed

Three established watersheds each received three independent random assignments of heritable marks. For each assignment we continued three checkpoint copies through 16,384 ticks of ordinary life. In the first, mark one read a visible neighbor's actual outline and mark two read an adjacent contact bank. In the second, the access assignments were reversed. Both marks read their normal contacts in the third copy. Food, migrations, construction, reproduction and all their downstream consequences continued in every fork.

Accurate audience access favored a lineage in **five of nine** reciprocal comparisons. The mean terminal share difference was **+2.54 percentage points** and individual differences ranged from **−12.88 to +18.65 points**. The nine comparisons cover three founder populations. These results establish that public sensory access can change the course of competition. A general survival advantage remains unestablished. ## Execution and reproduction

The field pathway uses no additional per-resident allocation. The optional public ledger occupies 7,168 bytes for the whole habitat. Native Blackwell executes the four-column Boolean responses with register-level byte permutations. Source sampling, synaptic updates, competing movements, hearing, pours and reproduction all run on CUDA. Full traces preserve 200 bytes of pre-release evidence per encounter. The ordinary evaluation seals the same decisions in **eight bytes**, then reduces outcomes by warp in a separate release kernel. It independently replays two detailed cases for the illustration. [Packed decisions](../include/axion/audience_batch.hpp) · [Detailed and compact parity](audience/packed-parity.csv)

On the RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell laptop, eight alternating measurements of 65,536 encounters gave a median **0.115 ms** for compact evaluation and **0.146 ms** for full tracing. These are CUDA event times for batches sharing two enrolled bodies. They exclude initialization, file I/O, image generation and the illustrative replay. The full million-site world runs at a different rate. Over 2,048 ticks it sustained **55.00 million site-ticks/s**, ending at tick 413,080 with **327,845 living creatures and 21,060,992 expressed cells**. During that interval 5,113 of 17,475 candidate social reaches were sensitive to which outline bank was read. The published checkpoint continued to tick **414,104** with **331,369 living creatures and 21,285,888 expressed cells**. Those last 1,024 ticks averaged **55.41 million site-ticks/s**. The median resident expressed 64 cells and the largest expressed 448, within a habitat capacity of 1,024 cells per individual. ## Research basis

Townrow and Krupenye tested whether bonobos signaled differently depending on their partner's access to hidden food. A 2026 caregiver experiment examined whether adults sample information to cover gaps in a child's object-label knowledge. Those experiments motivated varying the recipient's access while holding the guide's own experience fixed. Axion's private-periscope control tests a simpler behavioral explanation directly. [Bonobo communication experiment](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2412450122) · [Caregiver information sampling](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2026.106496)

Valet and colleagues studied arbitrary, socially transmitted and stable call usage across eight chimpanzee communities. Their November 2026 paper motivated measuring socially learned conventions separately from inherited signal order. Axion's two-voice test measures learning and persistence in a pupil. Transmission across generations of wild-speaking communities remains a separate experimental question. [Chimpanzee signal conventions](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2606837123)
