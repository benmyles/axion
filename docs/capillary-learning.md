# A creature explains a change and teaches another animal to build

At a bench, two carriers and a pad exchanged plum and teal. Someone installed both a wick and a film before releasing the second carrier. A watershed resident raised on one mineral answered “the wick let teal through.” The same animal raised on the other mineral answered “the film let teal through.” Both animals saw the same colours, material changes and final outcome. Their earlier hands-on experience supplied the distinguishing evidence. [Wick transcript](capillary-learning/craft-talk-0.txt) · [Film transcript](capillary-learning/craft-talk-16.txt)

A different resident could operate the six handles but had never been taught this four-action sequence. The guide said “lift wick upper left then place water upper middle then place water lower left then place wick lower right.” The pupil spent four units moving parts, set a net, and caught the next portion. This was the first case on the published instruction seed. The guide was born at tick 20,328, generation 114. The listener was born at tick 85,833 in a separate watershed, generation 214. Both had 64 active spiking cells at enrollment. [Physical and spoken record](capillary-learning/table-construction.txt) · [Living cohort screen](capillary-learning/education.csv)

## Experience, learning and what is built in

The animal has up to six local sensory afferents. Its genome supplies receptor disconnections, a three-to-six-input binding capacity, a Boolean synaptic update rule, the spiking program that admits updates, and weights for seeking consequences, uncertainty or novel combinations. A positive and a negative 64-bit contact plane record experience. Each world encounter can change the two contacts addressed by that animal's afferent volley. Two further words retain a local sight and a contrasting sight at the same location. The active pressure pathway adds 24 bytes per resident and four packed bits per habitat site. A vocabulary and learned motor grammar occupy space only for individually schooled speakers in a saved population. [Sensory and learning implementation](../include/axion/capillary.hpp) · [CUDA field](../src/flood_capillary.cuh)

The inference operator searches for the smallest sets of connected inputs that separate the two observed outcomes. It predicts an outcome only when the surviving models agree and supply evidence there. Translated bit masks test all 64 candidate input sets without building a table of pairs. In a fully identified material the circuit for all 64 heterogeneous sites of a workpiece is four 64-bit coefficient planes. At each time step, the GPU shifts in the west and north neighbours and evaluates a multilinear Boolean polynomial. A partially identified material uses separate bounds and propagates all locally possible pressures. The physical release kernel receives the hidden mineral. The animal's predictor receives its own contacts and visible materials. [Packed spatial circuit and supply accounting](../include/axion/capillary_sheet.hpp) · [Independent verifier](../tools/capillary_audit.py)

The artificial physics fixes what pressure, water, clay, fibre, dye and energy do. The material identification operator, six-lever search, sensory alphabet, candidate vocal projections and finite budgets are engineered priors. The particular material law, chosen experiments, construction, English vowel/consonant wave patterns associated with each referent and motor-to-word connections must be acquired or expressed by the animal. The tutor supplies linguistic demonstrations in the controlled experiments. English has not arisen spontaneously in this watershed. A maximum of six accessible handles bounds these construction results.

Three results from human research motivated the evaluation. Pan and colleagues reported that temporary perturbation of the human hippocampus disrupted transfer of learned relationships to new inferences. Goddu and colleagues found that toddlers could apply an observed relationship to bring about a new outcome. Pitt and colleagues studied children's induction of generative patterns. I used these as reasons to test transfer, deliberate intervention and recombination beyond the training length. The comparison motivates the task design. Generalization to human development remains untested. [Pan et al., November 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02627-w) · [Goddu et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2024.105959) · [Pitt et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-73029-9)

## Intervention, scarce stock and speech

In the initial 32 views the wick and coating covary. Opposite minerals therefore give identical passive observations. The resident can pay for eight interventions before seeing a new spatial configuration. At an assembled worksite, the wick and film drawers start empty under scarce supply. A piece can be lifted from another seat, and ordered operations can recover stock. Every handle either transfers one unit between installed and loose stock, or fails. Every batch includes both otherwise indistinguishable substrates, impossible cases and channels that need no work. The repair examiner independently releases all 64 physical configurations.

## In the freely reproducing habitat

Incoming pressure follows the local water depth, clay lip, material coating, regional mineral and seasonal spring and ebb. Animals can take a real piece of clay or fibre from their bodies, return one, or reorient loose stock. A pulse exchanges two actual dyes. A net consumes a share of existing litter if the pressure and outlet meet the animal’s expressed preference. Altering a supporting strand invalidates inherited reed and provenance markings through the same edge accounting used elsewhere in the watershed. No laboratory score is added to a wild animal's energy.

In a whole-estuary recording, handler 154,690 changed a clay sill at coordinate (295, 471). Nine pressure steps and 72 habitat ticks later, resident 168,247 ate three portions seven sites east and two south. Holding the subsequent field, springs, nets and animals' actual decisions fixed, the original emitted pulse made a difference at that meal. Of 132 pulse-changing interventions in the window, three affected another animal’s intake, accounting for six portions across the individual replays. A second whole-estuary window in the pigment-sorting condition had 390 pulse-changing interventions and zero credited portions. The observer follows a single altered pulse for up to ten local steps. Separate donors' effects can overlap. Intentional cooperation, propagation beyond the observed cone and the population effect over later generations remain unmeasured. [All sites in the first window's affected cones](capillary-learning/natural-cones.tsv.gz) · [Matched physical accounting](capillary-learning/propagation.csv)

Natural selection can also penalize handling. Over 65,536 additional ticks from a common small watershed, freely handling animals collected 44,378 portions through the sorting pathway. The observation-only population collected 213,300. Freezing the pressure contacts gave 19,882, and limiting forecasts to previously sensed inputs gave 4,872. These are whole-habitat counts, affected by population size, history, energy costs and other food sources. This experiment establishes neither a general fitness advantage for the new pathway nor the spontaneous evolution of technological culture. ## Did natural selection favour transferable learning?

Three starting populations each ran through four free-living treatments for a further 32,768 ticks. Each branch grew, reproduced and died under the habitat’s ordinary energy accounting. A separate examination sampled 2,048 living animals born during the last 2,048 ticks, with eyes and limbs, regardless of prior pressure experience. Every subject faced the same 24 held-out pairs of scarce-stock channels. We tested the same subjects with their pressure contacts retained and cleared. Clearing removed prior physical observations and preserved each body, genome and expressed somatic state.

| Population | Starting cohort | Free handling | Observation only | Fixed pressure contacts | Forecasts limited to experienced inputs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Brook watershed | 1,014 | 243 | 324 | 770 | 322 |
| Older watershed | 601 | 418 | 411 | 420 | 251 |
| Slow-pulse nursery | 1,066 | 586 | 941 | 399 | 435 |

Each number counts animals that learned the full local response of both materials in every trial, starting with cleared pressure contacts. All three free-handling populations declined on the common exam. At the brook, the number of distinct local synaptic update truth tables rose from 12 to 13. An earlier summary counted distinct 24-bit inherited words, which rose from 138 to 225. Only the low four bits select this two-input rule. The remaining bits and the separate somatic program must be considered according to their actual use in each circuit. The two younger starting populations shared ancestry. Animals within a branch are related, and sampling only living juveniles excludes nonsurvivors. These are descriptive treatment outcomes. A broadly capable selected guide and an ecology that maintains broadly capable learners are distinct achievements. The million-site estuary offered an especially difficult transfer. None of the 2,048 sampled coastal juveniles learned both full material laws across all cases. Only 114 committed to any construction, and the cohort earned 13 portions in 98,304 presentations. The sample carried 914 distinct inherited words covering all 16 local pressure-update truth tables. This census used the same seed, age window and exam as the three smaller studies. ## Execution and reproducibility

The continuing 1,024 × 1,024 habitat at tick 427,928 contains **316,127 living creatures and 20,302,656 expressed spiking cells**. The current median is 64 cells and the largest resident has 384, with a habitat capacity of 1,024 per body. Across this habitat's saved history, 593,846 recorded newborns exceeded their parent's cell count and 85 fell below it. The final 512 ticks advanced at **55.13 million site-ticks/s** on the NVIDIA RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell laptop GPU. The direct-pressure run from the same earlier estuary advanced at 55.02 million site-ticks/s, and the paired run with pressure off at 55.44 million. Each ratio uses reported simulation time. [Full-habitat timing](capillary-learning/habitat-timings.csv) · [Continuing estuary](../examples/capillary-estuary.flood.zst)

In a separate CUDA microbenchmark, 524,288 independent 8 × 8 sheets received fresh boundary pulses at each of nine steps. The answers from the per-site implementation and the compiled material circuit agreed on every bit of every final field, including 2,097,025 active sites. The median of seven timed passes was 2.93818 ms per-site, 0.06912 ms for the bounds circuit, and 0.043552 ms for the compiled 32-byte material, a 67.46× ratio between the first and last. These timings exclude host transfer, material compilation and training. Whole-world timings above include other physical and biological processes.
