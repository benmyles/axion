# Reading the evolution journal

Every completed run publishes a measured run plate and a separate creature card together. The journal maintains a current card, an illustrated timeline and its cross-run performance overview. The renderer and its fonts, drawing commands and encoder interface are embedded in the executable or supplied by the local system. A trained model or network connection is unnecessary for making a card. [Current creature card](../journal/creature-card.webp) · [The Axion journey](../journal/journey.webp) · [Journal and individual cards](../journal/JOURNAL.md)

**The creature card** names the focal specimen or sampled population. An individual checkpoint supplies its actual somatic cell count, installed circuits, sensory ports and acquired structures. The speaker in an interview is sampled again at the end. The roost records its highest-energy resident at the final census. Where an older record contains only a sampled island or habitat frame, the card identifies that sampling scope. A drawing of a body or lineage is a schematic encoding of the recorded body or roaming word.

**The local curve** uses one comparable measurement at a time. It can show mean learned productions per resident across seasons, mean expressed program length across habitat samples, or a brain measurement through checksum-linked checkpoint ancestry. An input and output inventory can show learning during a single run. These measurements can increase or decrease. [Six seasons of acquired productions](../journal/runs/00006526-imitate/creature-card.webp) · [A new procedural organ in one lifetime](../journal/runs/00006525-imitate/creature-card.webp)

**The project curve** counts distinct catalogued abilities with qualifying live evidence through the card’s run. Direct observations include movement, fruit harvested and calls invented. Scored probes require at least 16 trials and at least seven eighths correct. Some entries additionally require a structural comparison or physical replay. An archival table, a command-line option or the presence of a sensory port cannot, on its own, add a behavioral milestone. The count measures breadth of recorded evidence. Different creatures can contribute different abilities, and a later creature can lose one.

**The journey** starts at the first journal record, groups first demonstrations by run, marks peak observed habitat size and genealogy depth, and ends at the most recent record. Each landmark gives a run ID and a measurement. The cards for historical landmarks use only evidence available by their displayed run. The machine-readable timeline identifies the run and observation behind each ability. [Evidence index](../journal/capabilities.json)

```sh
./axion journal rebuild
make NATIVE=1 CUDA_ARCH=sm_120 test-journal
```

Rebuilding restores the root graphics, missing per-run cards and cards for older landmark records from the retained observations. Subsequent runs publish their own cards automatically. Existing historical cards keep their original horizon. WebPs are tracked with Git LFS.

The floodplain plate combines water, plant stock, clay banks, structural fibre and occupancy from the closing watershed. Its gold tracks follow up to eight sampled living individuals over the final 16 ticks. A track stops at death. The creature card reconstructs the actual 5 × 5 anatomy from its masks for body, limbs, pores and eyes. The body-size curve averages living residents at each census. The grounding plate retains both exposures and the caption from one recorded scene. A conversation plate can show spoken clarifications and physical pointing.

The journey is a **3840 × 2160 landscape WebP**. Read each chronological column from top to bottom, then move right. Its upper graph counts documented abilities and its lower strip identifies the latest timed watershed, or the latest timed workload when there has been no watershed run. A run plate and creature card report their own measured throughput, timing scope and resident state size when available. The floodplain also reports the share of its observation loop spent in advancing the simulation. That fraction includes launch, synchronization and the closing movement exposure and excludes subsequent checkpoint writes and image encoding. Older runs can recover the strip from recorded observations during `axion journal rebuild`. Input waiting is included in an interactive session's elapsed time.

## A population of field cards

The **3840 × 2160 creature atlas** ranks up to eight forms from the closing census. In the floodplain, a morphospecies is an identical expressed body mask and identical limb, eye and pore masks. Equal counts sort by those four masks. Every lobe and organ marker is drawn from the recorded geometry, and the short name describes the direction of a terminal branch. Gold rings identify small differences from the closest other form on the sheet. Genetically related animals can express different forms, and unrelated animals can converge on the same one. The cards retain their group counts, entry-to-close share, active brain and neural population ranges, total diet and the age, offspring and call exposure of the group's oldest living member.

The meadow, commons and roost also census their complete living populations. Those modes record body or locomotor codes and motor circuitry. Historical partial censuses are explicitly labeled as samples. An individual interview retains a separate profile. The global creature atlas follows the latest complete population census even when a later run is an individual interview. Each run keeps its own image.

For developing floodplain residents, the cell count on each card covers the current members of that body form. The oldest displayed member also has its own natal count, present count, lifetime maximum and arena capacity. When the feeding organ is present, the card shows the member's current four-muscle wiring and counts its nonzero temporal contacts. A milestone for working postnatal tissue requires a measured change in somatic motor choice during a silencing replay.

When the substrate contains reactive minerals, the run plate takes a local exposure around the largest measured connected component, or a photographed functional specimen when individual port tests are requested. Each grain’s orientation, terminal groups, stored excitation and actual incident bonds determine its glyph. The population cards show the oldest member’s carried grain, manual gates and registered work effects, which can include its own reinforced experience. Fluorescent coloring encodes the simulated state. The snapshot identifies the exposure tick and does not attribute intent to a structure.

Loose-stock construction plates show the original scattered pieces, the closing material field and measured harvests after the builders are removed. Their landscape creature cards retain the actual habitat census. Timing distinguishes simulated site-ticks, completed paired beds, and batches of predictive diagrams. [Construction experiments](shore.md)

Solvent habitats record the closing chemical mapping at each site in an eight-by-eight crop and the actual receptive fields of its focal resident. A cell's colour distinguishes its acquired two-bit state. The map above it identifies the soil that activates coating zero. The atlas lists expressed material-predicting cells and modified traces alongside the recorded body. Paired-bath plates use physical outcomes to compare lived, frozen, yoked, inverted and remapped evidence. Consultation plates count spoken advice, paid trials, correct names, errors and silence separately. [Material inference experiments](brine.md)


Clay workshop plates display the actual recorded note and pressure relief for each occupied groove. The field census can instead display the most densely imprinted grain with its exposure time and location. Population portraits give the oldest sampled member’s expressed and available context assemblies and the number of impressions on its carried grain. A source-attribution milestone requires externally scored answers and counterfactuals using an untouched object and a foreign object. [Physical records, mechanisms and measurements](clay.md)

The backwater plates show actual food withdrawals and local acoustic interventions. In a field map, the green bins divide fibre coverage into quarters. Amber dot sizes mark at least one eighth, one quarter or three eighths of local living residents with written bank contacts. In a paired probe, the plot follows first exposure, a hidden diversion and the return of the original spring. The supplementary generation chart tracks three independently founded populations across seven ages. [Generation chart](backwaters/generations.webp)
