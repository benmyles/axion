# Learning a watercourse by watching

A generation-26 forager learned four sounds for sequences of events. It can hear two of those sounds together, predict where a marked load will emerge, ask what happened when it could not see the outcome, and report an unexpected release. Its own English lessons, its parents' ecological history, and the trials on which it failed are preserved alongside the animal. ## What changed in the habitat

Moist fibre can carry water over a clay lip. Fastening or loosening strands at the four posts changes which lower channels and raised strands the water crosses, in what order, and where the food settles. Head, slope and substrate determine passage locally. The organisms retain their existing grip, muscles, mutable body plans, energy costs and ability to change brain size. A site can keep serving other animals after the builder dies. The hydraulic law defines an environmental building block. These runs did not test whether a builder foresaw the result.

Light from a passing glint reaches local retinas after feeding commitments. The code projects three pigment fields through the animal's eyes. Receptor alleles determine which changes excite its input rows. Two inherited truth tables determine somatic discharge and contact writes. Independent alleles address four local synaptic matrices by spectral context and route returning activity into the next stage. Watching costs an attending animal one energy unit. Shutters remove the final image, and displaced light shows the endpoint of a different inlet on the same bed. These controls preserve the cost of attending. ## How the speaking individual developed

1. Three broods each began with optical programs drawn from a living watershed. A nursery put those two mutable words into a common sensory and motor body. Observations there conveyed no food. Correct reaches intercepted a finite carbon bolus. Within each group of 32, carbon paid for offspring. In one treatment parental contribution followed food captured. In its control, any surviving adult could contribute uniformly. Each brood experienced 1,024 generations of up to 8,192 organisms. The nursery reused 830 wound beds sampled from a million-site estuary. The remaining 1,218 sites in the blind sample had a single stage. [Nursery stock](../examples/cascade-nursery.flood.zst) · [Common host](../examples/cascade-nursery-host.mind) · [Brood budgets](observational-learning/broods.csv)
2. On 1,024 trials using a different coastal population, each of the three food-selected circuits forecast all 1,024 outcomes in the same speaking host. Their uniform-parent counterparts scored 138, 41 and 35 for the same respective seeds. The foreign collection contained 2,048 physical sites, including 1,830 with multiple stages. Each trial gave the circuit raw local observations of single transfers before the combined flow. The same layout could be queried from each of its four inlets and in both orders. These trials measure transfer of a learned operation to new surfaces. Layouts are shared within pairs. [Nursery beds](observational-learning/estuary-beds.txt) · [Foreign coastal beds](observational-learning/coast-beds.txt) · [Uniform-parent speech](observational-learning/uniform-parents.tsv.gz)
3. Each optical germ was then placed in living founders in a shoal and an open-bottom habitat. The populations reproduced for 2,048 ticks. We sampled one descendant per habitat with a screening layout seed, taught each the same eight English nouns and eleven question forms, and examined it on separate layouts. Three of six voiced 1,022 correct forecasts in 1,024 coastal trials. All three named both outlets on each of the 127 order-sensitive pairs. The other three voiced zero forecasts. The published individual occupied slot 13,283, was born at tick 1,990 and expressed 64 spiking cells with four inherited module definitions. Its schooling began 58 ticks after birth. The nursery transfer took place in its ancestors. [Founding population](../examples/cascade-founders.flood.zst) · [Birth records](observational-learning/births.csv) · [Language lessons](../examples/inlet-lessons.txt)
4. A tutor gave each new word six demonstrations. Two equally bright posts flashed at every stage. Across demonstrations the relevant pigment recurred while its distractor varied. Only the public sound, visible pigment sets and the learner's own tissue entered the word circuit. The teacher's ordered gate numbers went to the apparatus. The lexical update was selected by the animal's inherited truth table and its auditory spiking gate. The public specimen resolved all four meanings, using 24 energy units. Its 54 stage exposures each admitted two candidate pigments. A second independently field-born pupil also learned all four. A third had enough available energy for twelve demonstrations and learned two words. [Exact lesson](../examples/cascade-lessons.txt) · [Glints, contacts and cortical spikes](observational-learning/word-exposures.txt) · [All teaching outcomes](observational-learning/lessons.csv)

The classroom saves the actual taught body, including the energy it spent. The watershed's existing return procedure transfers durable word contacts to the same verified living individual. The bounded paired examinations start from copies of that field tissue. The experimental speaker can replace several lamp names with one learned sound. The animal has to recover its meaning and propagate its own firing through the learned transitions.

## Does useful vision win in the wild?

The nursery paid offspring out of intercepted food. In the wild, thousands of other events also affect reproduction. We gave two heritable groups the same tissue and costs at the split, put them in competition and reversed which group saw the actual transition. The other group's endpoint came from a different source in the same bed. The sham gave both groups accurate glints. Each copy continued through ordinary birth, death, grazing and construction.

One of three exploratory founders produced opposite fixations under exchanged assignments. We then fixed six fresh founder seeds and a 65,536-tick endpoint. Informative sight improved terminal representation in **two of the six** independent founders. Its mean advantage in final population share was **−35.1 percentage points**. One founder showed nearly complete selection in its favor and one showed complete selection against it. This experiment establishes that toggling the information can change lineage survival. It provides no evidence for a consistent fitness advantage across these founder ecologies. The large swings in the two-informed shams show how easily one mark can dominate. ### Removing the remaining clue

Deliberately choosing a different inlet still tells the learner something. If a four-way route is reversible, seeing where a different inlet went excludes a possible answer. We enumerated the four actual inlets of each captured layer. Under uniform initial positions on the coastal collection, the real endpoint carries 1.462 bits about the inlet given the bed. Drawing a different inlet carries 0.263 bits. Drawing independently from all four carries zero. Geometry still gives some outlets higher base rates. [Exact source-information calculation](observational-learning/source-information.json)

We introduced the independent-source treatment in the live CUDA field and at the table. In the table test an independent permutation of endpoints preserves the exact spectrum of the four outgoing events in each observation block. The pupil scored 2,505/3,072 on real 21-stage histories, 742/3,072 with a different inlet, and 766/3,072 with an independent inlet. On captured coastal routes at the same exposure budget the scores were 3,064, 1,336 and 1,444. On the 385 paired coastal questions requiring opposite answers, it got both correct in 385, 41 and 49 cases respectively. [Captured independent-source glints](observational-learning/unpaired-glints.tsv.gz) · [All three treatments](observational-learning/spoken-forecasts.csv)

Repeating the reciprocal competitions with independent-source views in the same six founders gave positive terminal effects in two, negative effects in three and a tie in one. Mean share change was −16.1 percentage points. All six sham population trajectories reproduced exactly at every census. Neither the choice of foil nor the large cognitive benefit in the school predicts a reliable competitive advantage in these watersheds. ## GPU execution and storage

A resident uses four 16-contact matrices distributed over its existing hand and somatic gates. The optical updates operate on all 16 prospective contacts as bit fields. Teaching a temporal label reuses a vacant assembly in the resident's existing 568-byte discourse memory. The habitat adds no persistent bytes per creature for the new optical pathway. The optional optical ledger takes 13,312 bytes per habitat. Captured watercourses compile from 56 bytes of field evidence to four device bytes per tile. A diagnostic retinal exposure occupies four transferred bytes.

The paired examiner now computes a shared history once when two questions change only the subsequent order. Single-bed interventions with different observed histories still execute separately. Five alternating comparisons on the laptop used 7,705 resident circuits, 96 exposures and 1,024 twenty-one-stage forecasts per resident. Median confirmation time fell from **112.799 to 84.444 ms** for **7,889,920 forecasts**, or **93.4 million forecasts/s**. All ten full per-resident result tables agreed within their matched pairs. These are kernel timings for the held-out exam. In a separate 128-launch sample on the million-site habitat, the hybrid-accounting daylight kernel took a median **129.9 μs** per launch with **80 registers and zero local bytes** per thread. The core woven-flow kernel took **443.1 μs**, with **159 registers and zero local bytes** per thread. Enabling raw field recording uses a heavier instrumented variant. ## What the model assumes

Individual heads, muscles, optical receptors, bank addresses, local write rules and their metabolic consequences vary genetically. The test apparatus still supplies time boundaries, a small public vocabulary, distinct contexts, grouped demonstrations and a rule for reading a uniquely grounded sequence. Each temporal form can represent one to four pigment stages. Utterances can be combined up to 32 stages within this facility. The English question grammar and its referral to a remembered sight are bounded. Spontaneous naming between wild animals, unconstrained conversation and child-level intelligence remain open.

The experiments draw on distinct findings. Humans can acquire overlapping visual transitions as context changes. Axion's lamp-cued bank selection tests a simpler explicitly signaled case. Mouse timing experiments showed that the structure of early experience affects generalization. Here, matched food and uniform-parent broods isolate one contribution of a curriculum. A mapped sensory-cancellation circuit in electric fish motivated testing changes at multiple local stages. Human verb-learning experiments motivated using distracting referents that vary across demonstrations. The Axion results concern its own animals.
