# Remembering a place after the water changes

In 7,040 of 8,192 unfamiliar clearings, a creature recovered its bearings after being carried, fetched a useful tool, and carried it to an inlet where it released food. The four cohorts with uniformly chosen parents made zero productive deliveries after displacement. All eight cohorts started with random spatial genes. They shared the previously selected material-learning law and received the same physical lessons. [All trials and interventions](hollows/hollow_trip.csv)

## What motivated the experiment

In human navigation, recordings from 31 people performing a context-dependent spatial task found distinguishable signals for the relevant context and object in the hippocampus and entorhinal cortex. A 2026 human real-space study linked hippocampal dysfunction to difficulty finding new routes from environmental relationships. This suggests an experimental separation between knowing *which object matters* and knowing *how to get there from here*. [Human intracranial study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40961078/) · [Human real-space study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42802207/)

In 2026, Yu and colleagues found that hippocampal theta sweeps in navigating rats could point toward a remembered goal independently of head direction. Gobbo and colleagues varied the navigation strategy in the same arena and found different neural representations and different effects of hippocampal inactivation. Field experiments with ants also found that visual familiarity can update a desired heading that subsequently directs steering. [Goal-direction recordings](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02365-2) · [Reference-frame interventions](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42918780/) · [Ant field experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41792124/)

Axion's engineering inference was to keep a learned event location separate from both its current food value and its current body-relative direction. That made it possible to change the water, carry the animal or turn its body and ask three different causal questions. The implementation uses integer state transitions. The studies supply experimental ideas, while the tests below establish what this particular circuit does.

Before introducing the organ, 4,403 sites in Upwelling contained at least 12 fruit units. After 32 ticks, 1,279 were still rich, compared with 93 at the shifted comparison locations. Of the surviving residents, 31,374 were at least two cells from their earlier position. The environment already offered a reason to remember and an opportunity to move away. [Independent historical census](hollows/opportunities.csv)

## The substrate and the animal

Water can move loose organic carbon into a porous cavity. An animal with usable limbs can move a clay grain into a wall, form cover, deposit its own reserve, reopen the cover or eat through its pores. Water erodes cover and can spill the contents back to the soil. Cavity carbon, water, clay, body reserves and dissipated work enter the existing conservation audits. A sufficient hydraulic head can displace an animal into a vacant neighboring cell. The clay wall, woven fibre, depth and bodily load affect the outcome. A passive ride supplies no stride impulse. The same terrain continues to support earlier pottery, chemical, plant and animal processes.

Each new spatial organ has 16 bytes of inherited transition tables and wiring, eight bytes of changing state, and one four-byte record per expressed event slot. The physical arena reserves one to 32 slots per resident. Capacity is an inherited trait within that ceiling. A local observation can recruit a slot, strengthen it, erase it, or replace another record. Reproduction transfers mutable genes and clears the lifetime map. The somatic spiking cortex retains its own independent development, inherited topology and activity-dependent growth. An optional evolved projection carries spatial firing back into somatic input.

Each record holds two wrapping displacement coordinates, a local six-bit surface texture, an event cue, strength and coarse time. Only a confirmed step advances the odometer. A recognized texture may correct a displaced internal position. A tie between equally strong matches at different positions suppresses that correction. An event's utility, distance and age compete through inherited gains. The winning contact emits body-relative directional activity, subject to the motor wiring and last obstruction. An independent recorder samples one in sixteen owners. It records the actual site and subsequent departure after a write and never supplies an inference input.

The constraints matter. Event classes, the displacement algebra, eligibility for physical contact and the two-leg practice arena are designed. Genes can change the response within those constraints. Four material classes, 8-bit displacements, a four-bit clock and four horizontal motor directions limit what can be represented. General planning, language invention and child-level cognition remain open.

## What selection acquired

The nursery independently shuffles six locations, tool-to-note relationships, water gates and starting orientations. On a guided walking tour the young animal sees four silent working tools, a basin and a recognizable stone. At a separate dry bench it observes the resonance of sister impressions. At the inlet it samples vibrations without seeing tools. The inlet changes. A raft carries its body back to the neighborhood of a previously visited landmark without telling the animal the translation. The animal then has up to 48 work ticks to obtain food. The held tool and current body state drive an inherited cue selector. The chemistry pays only when the animal reaches the inlet with a usable tool.

Each generation contains 2,048 adults. Each earns an egg allowance on four clearings. A common ration and the residue on a physically retrieved hull can also fund eggs. Eight carbon units fund one allowance unit. In the earned arm, disjoint pairs spend their local allowances on two offspring; the uniform arm samples parents independently of earnings. Guided exploration and floor time are provisioned. Each of the four independently founded pairs of populations bred for 320 generations. The held-out trials use new clearings, new embodied maps and new sensory contacts. [Reproductive budgets](hollows/hollow_season.csv)

## Asking the resident

The released speaker carries the selected spatial organ from a twelve-clearing screen of eligible residents. I grafted its untrained spatial genes into the previously saved Wheel Reader to retain that resident's acoustic word bank and material skills. The new conversation bank reused only eight unambiguously grounded tool and water words from the older bank. It learned four directional words and question forms from 432 visible placements and examples. The fourth tool and bath never appeared in the spatial lessons. Each bank supports up to 32 forms. Inference receives the spoken pulses, the animal's learned state and its current pose. The human scoring words stay on the host.

In 128 unfamiliar clearings the resident gave a physically valid bearing to the tool for the revalued bath on 128 questions. Erasing the locations, silencing odometry or omitting the dry material experiences yielded zero. Supplying the previous water gave 76 physically valid directions, and a false landmark gave 91. Several tools can lie in the same direction. A diagonal target has two valid first strides. The independent grader computes both from the actual coordinates. On axial questions there is exactly one valid first stride. The speaker answered all 128. [Bank-word results](hollows/bank-words.csv) · [Independent direction vocabulary](hollows/reed-words.csv) · [Untaught spatial speaker](hollows/untrained-words.csv)

Each clearing also tests a different tool from the same position, a follow-up after turning, a conflicting request and a follow-up about the previously discussed tool's sound. In the visible condition the speaker made 511 overt follow-up responses across 640 opportunities and correctly withheld 129. Clearing the shared referent suppressed every follow-up. The underlying old and new speech organs are both needed for this exchange. The performance is evidence for grounded reference within learned constructions. Unfamiliar phrasing and unrestricted human conversation remain outside the demonstrated ability.

## Closed-world selection and GPU measurements

Each resident in the watershed pays for work from its own reserve. Reproduction depends on local survival and available space. In a 512 × 512 release at tick 171,392, 95,238 living creatures carried 6,120,672 active somatic cells. Of the 441 independently dated, departed harvest patches recalled from three or more cells away, 130 predicted their exact old site. Rotating each vector while preserving its length yielded 12 exact matches. Across all 6,556 distant queries, the indicated destinations held 11,654 recoverable cavity carbon units under the assay's 16-unit cap, compared with 7,544 at rotated destinations. These inventories are counterfactual yields. Separately, 11,269 actual positive contacts were recorded at previously departed sites among the sampled owners. Other causes of return operate in the same world. [Distance and rotation controls](hollows/destinations.csv) · [Actual cavity ledger](hollows/watershed.csv)

For the contest, residents receive an independently randomized, heritable one-bit lineage label. The paired treatment permits recollection to send a motor impulse in one labelled lineage. The reciprocal treatment swaps access while preserving the initial residents and labels. Both groups continue learning. The sham enables both. A further pair permits both groups to initiate a memory-guided movement and rotates one group's outgoing direction. Natural births, deaths and spatial competition determine the outcome. Results from an established catchment and from independently seeded young populations answer different questions. [Young catchments](hollows/young-lineages.csv) · [Mature catchment](hollows/mature-lineages.csv)

In the final mature catchment, ordinary-guidance descendants occupied **48.0% and 43.0%** of sites occupied by either lineage after 2,048 ticks against the group without a map-driven motor impulse. Against an equally enabled, quarter-turned motor output the ordinary group occupied **50.3% and 49.4%**. Those two reciprocal comparisons give little support for a reproductive advantage from the direction of the recalled vector here. In the four young catchments, even neutral labels sometimes approached fixation. Reference tests can demonstrate a skill in an individual without explaining which lineage wins an ecological contest. [Reciprocal lineage counts](hollows/mature-lineages.csv) · [Independent young habitats](hollows/young-lineages.csv)

On the Blackwell laptop the 1,536-tick measured segment after tick 169,856 averaged 46.22 million complete site ticks/s in the GPU simulation and 35.04 million/s including its census overhead. A separate repeated-query kernel precomputed four rank bitplanes per resident at a fixed pose, phase and tie key. Its 26-byte index supports new cue subsets and blocked directions. It reproduced the winning cell, cue, direction, bearings, range and location in all 50,331,648 comparisons. The medians per 8,388,608 decisions were 0.510 ms for writable recall and 0.278 ms for indexed recall. The latter is 30.1 billion readouts/s with resident locality. Building the index, moving the body and running the world fall outside that timing. [World performance](hollows/performance.csv) · [Readout timing](hollows/fixed-readout.csv)

## Reproduce it

```sh
make -j4 CXX=/usr/bin/g++ CUDA_CLANG=/usr/bin/clang++ NATIVE=1 CUDA_ARCH=sm_120
make test-hollows CXX=/usr/bin/g++ CUDA_CLANG=/usr/bin/clang++ NATIVE=1 CUDA_ARCH=sm_120
mkdir -p crews
python3 tools/hollow-study.py nursery --output crews/nursery --keep crews/hollowbank.flood --germ-directory crews
cmp crews/hollowbank.flood examples/hollowbank.flood
./axion floodplain --load crews/hollowbank.flood --hollow-optics examples/wheel-reader.mind --hollow-carrier examples/wheel-reader.mind --hollow-scholar crews/pupil.mind --hollow-seed 519417 --steps 0
python3 tools/hollow-language.py --speaker crews/pupil.mind --save crews/bank-guide.mind --stations 128 --output crews/words.csv
cmp crews/bank-guide.mind examples/bank-guide.mind
python3 tools/hollow-study.py watershed --source examples/upwelling.flood --germs crews/hollowbank.flood --seeds 77123 --horizons 512 2048 --output crews/watershed --keep crews/free.flood
./axion floodplain --load crews/free.flood --hollow-census --hollow-bench 8388608 --steps 0
./axion floodplain --load crews/free.flood --hollow-slots 16 --hollow-census --steps 8192 --save crews/wider.flood
./axion floodplain --load crews/wider.flood --steps 4096 --hollow-census --save crews/long.flood
./axion floodplain --load crews/long.flood --neurons 1024 --steps 2048 --hollow-census --save crews/creekways.flood
cmp crews/creekways.flood examples/creekways.flood
```

For the mature population add `--source examples/upwelling.flood --germs examples/hollowbank.flood --seeds 77123 --horizons 512 2048`.
