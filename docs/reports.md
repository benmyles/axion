# Telling another animal what happened

A witness sees two resources move. Its partner has closed shutters. The witness can learn a sequence such as **water moved from south to west**, speak it on a new occasion, and allow the partner to reconstruct the concealed movement. The listener can also take a human statement as evidence and express part of it in a differently learned word order. The older four-bearing spatial circuit is still available to the same individual. [Recorded conversation](../journal/runs/00004587-talk/plate.webp) · [Witness](../examples/storyteller.mind) · [Listener](../examples/confidant.mind)

## Design and research

Silva and colleagues reported on November 19, 2026 that a region of human posterior superior temporal cortex carries information about upcoming words before articulation. Duraivel and colleagues found different time courses for planning and executing speech units in human intracranial recordings. These findings motivated a distinction in Axion between planning a word from available evidence and emitting its learned acoustic pattern. Axion implements a symbolic motor channel. Each transmitted form is a packed sequence of letter addresses. [Silva et al., Science](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aef8963) · [Duraivel et al., Nature Human Behaviour](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-026-02546-w)

Shin and colleagues reported on November 18, 2026 that spatial and temporal context contributed to episodic memory in a task involving objects placed in a virtual environment. In wild bonobos, Berthet and colleagues found evidence that combinations of calls carry meanings related to their components. Our engineering question was whether an individual with a small private event memory could learn which pieces of an event each part of an utterance describes, and whether that information helps another individual act. [Shin et al., Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-79267-1) · [Berthet et al., Science](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40179186/)

The reporter adds **744 bytes** to an individual. A changed retinal channel supplies the object, departure bearing and arrival bearing. An inherited gate decides how stable views, single moves, simultaneous changes and incomplete views affect two event records. Each position in an utterance receives inputs from those records and a constant input. Its contacts can associate a heard form with a feature value. A mutable table controls the writes and a second table changes confidence according to local agreement or surprise. Competing inputs release a word-sized pulse when they agree on its acoustic address. A learned stop pulse can end the utterance at different positions.

The body contains two cue-addressed constructions, up to eight successive word pulses and a bank of 24 acquired acoustic forms of up to twelve letters each. A heritable policy can replace a construction, and unreferenced sound slots can be reclaimed when a demonstration needs space. The GPU sends the actual acoustic forms across the channel. Each listener uses its own contacts to constrain a 32-bit set of possible movements for each event age. A retelling can supply a word only when its possible interpretations agree. Visual history and testimony occupy separate state. [Speech circuit](../src/report.cuh) · [State layout](../include/axion/report.hpp) · [Public sensor and motor ports](../src/dialogue.cu)

## What the pair learned

Eight selection populations and eight drift populations began with randomized speech programs. Each population had 256 candidates, eight worlds per candidate and 192 generations. In each world, a writer and a reader acquired their contacts from different demonstrations. The classroom excluded one direction of displacement during tuition, then used it for testing. It varied word order, sound labels, optional endings and which of the two events the message described.

On the matched population audit, **1,222/2,048** selected individuals reached the maximum score, compared with **0/2,048** drift individuals. The released program came from the prespecified first selection seed, 98117. On a separate 4,096-trial evaluation its utterances matched all 4,096 targets, and the separately reared readers recovered all 4,096 private events. Muting or reversing transmission gave zero correct readings. Systematically displacing the teaching labels for both partners gave zero exact matches to the original convention while preserving 4,096 correct readings between the two animals. [Population results](reports/populations.csv) · [Classroom interventions](reports/classroom.csv)

The independent public examiner used only scene placement, spoken demonstrations, queries, and the receiving animal's motor readout. It withheld one displacement class throughout rearing. On eight fresh seeds, independently taught pairs spoke and decoded **384/384** English reports and **384/384** reports in new dialects. The new dialects included a seven-word report of both events and a shorter construction with an optional ending. Muting or reversing the same transmissions gave zero correct readings in both tests. In ordinary English the released pair also recovered the requested recent or previous movement in **720/720** questions covering all 360 physically compatible adjacent movement pairs. [English](reports/held-english.csv) · [Novel dialects](reports/held-novel.csv) · [Complete spoken movement test](reports/all-motions.csv)

With vision covered, a listener extracted the latest movement from a longer statement and expressed it in its own shorter construction on **192/192** trials. Exchanging only the departure and arrival in the incoming description gave **192/192** corresponding changes in the retelling. Given only the shorter description, the listener withheld all **192** requests for the missing previous movement. [Sound-only counterfactuals](reports/held-exchange.csv)

## Dialects in the commons

Each resident witnesses movements in its own part of an island. To obtain food, a resident must set a trap for the object, departure and arrival in its neighbour's sector. Ordinary actions and vocal costs consume energy, reservoirs are finite, and reproductive allocation depends on material acquired during life. A resident can pay for a visit, see the event behind a neighbour's call and update its own contacts. Every new generation hatches without the acquired speech contacts. An island can transmit a convention through an adult's demonstrations, and tutors can be drawn in proportion to reproductive deposits. [World and acoustic exchange](../src/culture.cuh)

In a cross-fostering test, genetically identical newborns on alternating islands encountered two different tutors. The same canonical event elicited **water moved from north to east** on some islands and **mepo tesu jori zuna rika** on others. Across eight gardens, listeners recovered **524,288/524,288** private movements with the paired channel open. Disconnecting the channel, reversing its words or omitting tuition gave zero correct readings. Redirecting a different neighbour's message gave 55,050 correct readings among 436,560 definite interpretations. The intact residents deposited 3,570,696 energy units in offspring. [Cross-fostering and controls](reports/cross-fostering.csv) · [Alternate tutor](../examples/controls/island-tutor.mind)

In the invention condition the report vocabulary and acquired construction fields start empty. Inherited connections can produce provisional syllable patterns from currently active sensory features. The genes influence which features can drive each pulse, pulse order, sound assignment and length. A founder can repeat the same feature, omit useful information or emit a constant. No English labels or prescribed meanings for the invented syllables enter this habitat. Newborn listeners have to acquire their interpretations through sound and experience. This is a factorized perceptual and motor bias with a finite capacity.

After 32 generations with a cost of one energy unit per emitted word, a resident and its actual neighbour were exported from the selected family. In the public interview the neighbour identified the **latest** movement in all 360 compatible two-movement histories. Muting the channel gave 0/360 and reversing its words gave 3/360. Varying the physical events revealed the following structure in the five-word code. Its third and fourth pulses redundantly conveyed the earlier destination. The score measures recovery of the latest event. [All emitted forms and readings](reports/wild-interview.csv) · [Slot meanings recovered from observations](reports/invented-positions.csv) · [Residents and sampled dialects](../journal/runs/00004293-commons/plate.webp)

## Learning without a tutor

A second lineage received **zero demonstrations** at birth. It began with the earlier evolved learning program and empty report vocabularies. Peers could babble, spend energy to visit, and associate what they saw with what they had heard. Over 48 generations, deposited energy affected genetic recruitment. The released newborn again contains zero acquired report forms. [Breeding history](reports/field-breeding.csv) · [Newborn checkpoint](../examples/self-taught-newborn.mind)

Each row below covers eight fresh gardens with 1,024 residents and up to 128 days apiece. The selected newborn, its drift control and its ancestor each entered the volatile test without a tutor. Under volatile conditions the island's prevailing motion changes during life.

Freezing auditory learning during paid visits or muting the peer channel reduced reproductive deposits to zero in both selected gardens. The exported mature pair from the untutored lineage identified only **40/360** latest movements in the unrestricted interview. Its convention worked much better in the ecology in which it acquired it. ## Blackwell measurements

On the RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell laptop GPU, **1,048,576** independent 256-cell residents learned from **100,663,296** spoken demonstrations in **176.266 ms** of CUDA event time. The next 32 days took **598.586 ms**, including the somatic neural updates, private observations, utterances, decoding, hunting and competition. The residents emitted 184,547,333 words and made 33,553,801 correct definite readings. The application allocated **1,650,378,508 device bytes**. Hatching, host reporting and image encoding fall outside the two measured intervals. [Full-world measurement](reports/scale-million.csv) · [Million-resident record](../journal/runs/00004508-commons/plate.webp)

Neighbouring CUDA lanes exchange packed sound patterns through warp shuffles. A speech column stores its compiled seven-bit input selection and a validity bit in an unused byte of an existing contact word. Online learning refreshes that selection. On 65,536 residents, rearing through a thread-private working copy took a median **9.586 ms** versus **21.682 ms** using direct global updates. With the private-copy layout, 32 subsequent days took 38.730 ms with cached masks and 39.416 ms with direct selection. All three layouts produced identical outcomes. [Timing samples](reports/layout.csv)

## Reproduce

```sh
make -j4 NATIVE=1 CUDA_ARCH=sm_120 all test-gpu
./axion narrate --origin random --generations 192 --candidates 256 --worlds 8 \
  --cycles 8 --exposures 64 --probe-worlds 512 --seed 98117 --probe-seed 2049101 \
  --save writer.mind --save-listener reader.mind
python3 tools/report-study.py rear --speaker writer.mind --listener reader.mind \
  --exposures 160 --save-speaker speaking.mind --save-listener listening.mind
python3 tools/report-study.py interview --language novel
python3 tools/report-study.py exchange
python3 tools/report-study.py census --both
./axion talk --speaker examples/reed-speaker.mind --listener examples/reed-listener.mind
```

The two ecological founder experiments can be reproduced independently.

```sh
./axion commons --load examples/storyteller-newborn.mind --channel reports \
  --culture invent --founders varied --policy harvest --families 256 --replicates 8 \
  --days 96 --generations 32 --tutoring 48 --reserve 24 --rations 12 --calories 8 \
  --report-cost 1 --volatility 0 --seed 82139 --save island.mind --save-neighbor peer.mind
./axion commons --load examples/storyteller-newborn.mind --channel reports \
  --culture invent --founders varied --policy evolved --families 256 --replicates 8 \
  --days 128 --generations 48 --tutoring 0 --reserve 48 --price 1 --rations 12 \
  --calories 8 --report-cost 0 --volatility 0 --seed 741193 --save-newborn unschooled.mind
```

The model still relies on a small set of sensory features, two event records and two learned constructions. It can report and retell events in this setting. Open-domain conversation, unrestricted concept formation and child-level cognition remain open goals.
