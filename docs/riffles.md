# Riffles

A creature can hold its position at a mineral patch, try another handle, pick fibre out of loose litter, and fit it between two pieces. A connection can divert water and food to another animal born later. Across the larger landscape, wet fibre can also carry dissolved carbon beyond the four pieces that a single creature can directly model.

Two animals in the studio can now resolve a different problem. The speaker knows two sets of material dynamics. The worker faces an apparatus that looks the same in both. The speaker can ask the worker to move a particular handle and watch a particular component, hear what happened, and give a different procedure depending on the answer.

## Hear a real exchange

```sh
./axion talk --speaker examples/gauge-reader.mind --listener examples/causeway-guide.mind < examples/gauge-dialogue.txt
```

The script presents the same assembly and request twice. The worker’s two physical calibrations have identical public markings. In both cases, the speaker asks *turn slate pool face flint*. This means to operate the pool-marked handle on the slate part and report the face of the flint part.

| Worker’s actual report | Speaker’s subsequent procedure |
| --- | --- |
| `face flint rill` | `go ivory slate amber brook slate pool` |
| `face flint pool` | `go amber slate slate brook slate brook` |

The first two nouns following *go* name a fibre connection. The remaining noun and handle pairs name strokes. The two procedures attach different pieces. Each worker decodes the public acoustic pulses through its own prior vocabulary and its own actuator ordering. The worker pays for the test even when the report is muted. A successful delivery releases 40 units from a finite food reserve. [Playable fixture and questions](../examples/gauge-dialogue.txt)

With the same two individual files, start `axion talk` without input redirection to ask your own questions. Use `/studio 2516823844 4` and `/studio-layout 4240463554 0 4` for the first calibration. `/studio-order` reads the physical lock. `choose PART FACE ...` requests a configuration. `/studio-pass` sends the current question or procedure to the worker. `why` asks which piece the speaker needs to inspect. `why flint pool` asks what it would do after that possible report, without performing the action. A new `choose` after a paid test revises the target while preserving the speaker’s evidence and the worker’s remaining budget.

The released reader carries two learned material memories for that studio world. Its pre-exposure counterpart is `examples/gauge-student.mind`. To teach the student in a different world, open `/studio SEED 4`, enter `/studio-study 2`, change the actual substrate with `/studio-rain`, then enter `/studio-study 2` again. ## How a creature decides to test

A four-faced material with reversible handles admits six distinct directed cycles through its visible colours. A context stores the surviving alternatives for four material types in 24 bits. A stroke produces a before–action–after observation. The gene for combining old activity with new evidence contains a four-entry Boolean table. A second contact population learns whether a connection engages at each visible face, which direction it drives its neighbor, and what physical connection results from fitting fibre. One context costs 20 bytes. The inherited bank capacity ranges from one to four contexts within the allocated arena.

An observation can discredit the currently active context. Compatible prior contexts can be recalled, and the animal can recruit a free context when the earlier ones fail. No climate number enters the learner. In the field, a separately heritable attention circuit can maintain a bout of work, inhibit a voluntary departure, and stop when the encounter becomes expensive. Two-bit contacts associate an observed resource and uncertainty condition with staying or leaving. Genes set their updates and determine which feedback events affect the circuit. Direct and witnessed contacts can have different effects. Young inherit the 16-byte practice genome and start with empty private models. The earlier somatic growth mechanism continues to govern birth size, growth and cell loss. [Evolvable somatic cell counts](neurogenesis.md)

For an order, the studio speaker first searches for a common procedure under its learned models. It can also test a handle and ask for one visible face. Models predicting the same report must share a subsequent procedure. The speaker compares the predicted work of these options. A real report can narrow its working set of models before a revised goal. The reversible-cycle prior, observation grammar and bounded search are designed mechanisms. The creature still has to acquire the dynamics, ask an informative question and act on the resulting evidence. The studio supports four pieces, one loose connector and up to twelve strokes per continuation.

## Why these experiments

A study of 18- to 24-month-old children found that experimentally encouraging alternative actions increased persistence during problem solving. A separate mouse study found longer task-like structure within spontaneous behavior. These findings motivated giving a creature control over the duration and contents of a work bout, then testing timing independently of the number of actions available. [Exploration supports toddlers’ persistence during problem-solving](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42683899/) · [Spontaneous behavior is a succession of self-directed tasks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41610841/)

Mice have been shown to sacrifice water to obtain information even when it cannot improve the external reward. In human motor adaptation, cues, recency and stability affected which competing motor memory participants expressed. Axion’s tests distinguish the cost of seeking evidence, its physical usefulness, and the consequences of using another memory. Fitness receives food actually obtained. A reported reduction in uncertainty contributes no calories. [Representations of the intrinsic value of information in mouse orbitofrontal cortex](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02377-y) · [Contextual cues and transition statistics drive expression of competing motor memories](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13355677/)

## Can a fixed instruction explain the success?

An independent program generated visually indistinguishable pairs and enumerated action sequences on their product state space. It allowed strokes on every accessible handle and an optional, irreversible fitting at **any** place in the sequence. The budget was three to six units of work. A diagnostic pair qualified only if each target could be reached, no fixed sequence within budget could reach both, and at least one physical probe could produce distinguishable observations with feasible continuations. These conditions were calculated outside CUDA and withheld from both animals.

Of 318 candidate fixtures, the generator retained 256 paired requests in each of three classes. The classes were information-dependent, solvable by a common program, and impossible in both calibrations. The intact speaker and worker completed **223 of 256 information-dependent pairs in both worlds**. In every one of these 223 pairs the initial utterance was identical, the reports differed and the successful procedures differed. Muting or falsifying the report reduced paired success to zero. On these 512 individual presentations, the creatures completed 446 orders and fitted 82 loops. There were 162 diagnostic strokes whose requested observation concerned a component other than the one being touched. [Paired outcomes](inquiry/spoken-pairs.csv) · [Independent physical replay](inquiry/spoken-replay.csv) · [Every public utterance, change and payment](inquiry/spoken-trace.tsv.gz)

In a separate test the requested target changed during the exchange. The physical escrow was preset to the final target. The speaker learned of the revision through the second utterance. On 128 information-dependent pairs, the two residents completed **88 pairs in both calibrations** after the amendment. These measurements use fixed learned role names and finite material tasks. Free conversation, self-created language and domain-general planning remain outside the demonstrated result. ## Natural selection and opportunity costs

The opening Millrace census found 75,593 of 86,046 living animals with zero recorded strokes. In a matched 1,024-tick continuation, scheduling an ordinary workshop every tick instead of every fourth tick left just five residents with 32 or more observations. This motivated testing voluntary bouts at the original workshop frequency. An engaged animal can spend additional ticks at the object. An inherited actuator still has to operate, every stroke costs real energy, and staying can prevent a useful departure. [Every-tick census](inquiry/interval-1.csv) · [Every-fourth-tick census](inquiry/interval-4.csv)

In controlled selection, 2,048 juveniles bred for 256 generations per replicate. Earned and uniform populations encountered the same conditions. Elementary material practice and held-out work were separate. Across three seeds the fixture-selected populations completed 1,568 of 3,072 unfamiliar four-part orders in the unknown-material test. Matched uniform reproduction produced 40 completions. Reproduction on a finite four-patch foraging island alone produced 122 completions. Combining foraging and elementary fixture experience produced 1,533. The independent island-only populations also made 2,505 intended spring openings during the foraging assay, compared with eight for uniform reproduction. The nursery objectives select different competencies. [Reproductive budgets](inquiry/reproduction.csv) · [Unfamiliar material tests](inquiry/unknown-material.csv) · [Finite foraging accounts and interventions](inquiry/foraging.csv)

On the finite island, each location retains its remaining loose fruit, concealed carbon and physical arrangement between visits. A forager can leave, consume loose resources, manipulate a handle or alter a connection. Earlier frontier calibration runs encountered fresh patches on each departure. Their selected populations often earned more by traveling nearly continuously. [Finite and frontier foraging](inquiry/foraging.csv)

To isolate timing, copies of the same resident foraged on two arrangements. We replayed their own schedules and exchanged schedules, ending all four trials at the same surviving tick. Across three independently selected populations, each treatment made **144,958 stays and 137,088 departures**. Keeping the locally chosen timing left **190,643** carbon in reserve. Exchanging the schedules left **111,760**. Local timing finished ahead in 2,380 of 3,072 pairs, behind in 633, and tied in 59. Uniformly bred controls retained 120,475 carbon with local timing and 122,446 with exchanged schedules. The selective advantage measured here belongs to the finite nursery. ## What happened outside the nursery

A separate branch placed random practice genes and **no classroom experience** in the existing Millrace population. Its body lineages, acquired linkage contacts and physical territory continued from the saved world. Within 4,096 ticks its residents installed 20 strands under explicit predictions of a working opening. An observer recorded the actual installer, birth time, tick and physical fibre edge. Later physical changes to that edge invalidate the attribution. Every claim of necessity recomputes the channel with that single unit of fibre removed at the same physical state. The animals cannot read this record.

At 83 open observations in that unassisted branch, the strand was physically necessary. Such channels transferred 100 units of carbon, delivered 23 units to other residents over 12 meals, and fed seven times at works older than the eater. In a matched branch seeded with the fixture-selected practice genes, the corresponding results were 148 deliberate installations, 642 necessary openings, 681 units transferred, and 68 feeding events for other bodies. Twenty of those meals used works older than the eater. Removing all nine surviving attributed strands at the sampled checkpoint returned nine units of fibre to loose litter. An independent reader verified all 262,144 soil records and 18,874,368 private practice bytes. The next 512 ticks yielded no additional carbon on a necessary strand in either the retained or stripped branch. [Attribution records](inquiry/attribution.csv) · [Independent removal check](inquiry/strand-removal.csv)

The released world follows the unassisted branch. By tick 232,832 it had recorded 23 deliberate installations in total. Sixty living residents still had at least 32 practice observations. Most of the installations occurred early. Across two founding labels and their reciprocals, the group entitled to initiate sustained work finished with 21.8%, 24.4%, 25.6% and 26.4% of living residents after 4,096 ticks. In the two shams both groups had access, and their numbers remained much closer. In this ecology a public benefit coexisted with a disadvantage to the lineage bearing its costs. It also contains no evidence that a resident designed a long pipeline for a future benefit. ## A landscape larger than the private planner

Wet fibre connects neighboring mineral interfaces. Relative saturation determines the direction of flow. The material faces, fibre, mineral mass, existing banks and cavity relief determine conductance. Moving water carries finite carbon between pore stocks or surface litter. Transfers into or out of hollows debit and credit the existing sediment accounts. A resident can gather loose litter into its body and lay a strand across a workshop boundary. Local transfers can compose over the entire periodic grid.

Passive tracer labels follow real capillary transfers. They have no energetic value and never supply information to a creature. The field stores a conserved identity, a saturating hop count and two body-made edge flags in one 32-bit word per site. After an expansion, travel distance is evaluated on the initial periodic grid so inserting land at a seam does not become artificial tracer travel.

A controlled experiment used 64 strips, each 64 mineral sites long. The continuous strips delivered all 303,104 initial units of carbon to the terminal reservoirs. Cutting the middle strand stopped that delivery. Using one retained unit of fibre per strip to repair the break at the midpoint restored all 303,104 units. The experimenter prepared these strips. In the inhabited watershed, the first 2,048 ticks of wicking moved 326,454 carbon units across workshop boundaries. Preventing cross-boundary flow reduced that count to zero. Across the released trajectory the capillaries moved **1,589,471** units of carbon, including **896,139** through edges residents had newly supplied with fibre. Of 8,193 conserved tracers, 983 had made at least two transfers and the farthest finished six sites from its source. ## GPU execution and replay

Private practice state costs 32 bytes per site including its genome, plus 20 bytes per allocated context. A deliberate-strand record costs four bytes per site averaged over each four-site patch. Wicking adds four bytes per site when enabled. All learning, reproduction, probing, public language and material transfers execute in CUDA.

A one-stroke forecast can read a compact context directly. On the Blackwell laptop, 262,144 forecasts took **0.0473 ms** using direct contacts and **0.1991 ms** after expanding complete transition charts. For repeated contingent work, a warp scan packs only the reachable continuations of each policy. A page has one 32-bit base and 128 local 16-bit offsets. In the measured set, 529 actual genomes yielded 225 conditional policies, 56 fixed policies and 248 refusals. Their action images occupied **1,799 bytes plus 1,078 bytes of directory**. Compiling the collection took 48.74 ms. For 262,144 reports, generating decisions anew took 1,241.01 ms and dispatching the packed decisions took 0.0441 ms. Both comparisons matched across 1,048,576 outputs. These are device-event intervals for a fixed collection of learned fixtures and goals. A change of goal, visible geometry, eligibility or beliefs can require a new policy. [Direct contact benchmark](inquiry/direct-forecasts.csv) · [Conditional action image](inquiry/branch-image.csv)

The final 4,096 ticks of the 512-square watershed advanced at **42.38 million complete site-ticks/s**. A 1,499 plus 2,597 tick split restart and a second uninterrupted run at 43.51 million site-ticks/s each reached the same checkpoint bytes. Replaying the entire **14,336 ticks** from the earlier Millrace release also reproduced every byte. The watershed contains **83,008 living creatures**, **5,331,744 active somatic spiking cells**, and **6,785 observed body forms**. A separate 1,024-square expansion was relatively sparse and measured 110.32 million site-ticks/s over 128 ticks.
